# Listening & Learning: new looks (concept stills)

All stills show the "It is my belief" moment (17.5 s) at 1080 x 1920, made vertical, inside the TikTok
safe area, with the real caption. Made by `source/ll_looks.py` (`stills` and `sheet`). The film itself
has not been re-rendered.

## What the current look shares with FilmCow
- Dot or tiny almond eyes in a big blank face, doing all the acting.
- One even, clean black outline round every shape.
- Flat fills with no texture (only a soft airbrushed shadow).
- Simple block construction: a rectangle neck, a box tee, tube arms and legs.
- A still, plain, symmetrical staging and a sincere, flat delivery.

What we actually need: the still camera, the plain staging and the sincere delivery (that is the deadpan),
plus bold readable shapes on a phone. The eyes, the outline, the flat fills and the construction are
FilmCow's signature and can go.

## The five looks
1. **Coloured pencil** (`pencil.png`): pencil strokes on warm paper, outlines in a darker pencil of each
   colour, blue-iris almond eyes. Keeps the essence by staying plain and still; ties to Dam and The Salt.
2. **Two-colour riso** (`riso.png`): pink and blue ink only, halftone dots, off-register, no outlines,
   heavy half-closed lids, a big square head. Flat and graphic, so fast and bold on a phone.
3. **Paper cut-out** (`cutout.png`): cut card with real shadows, torn-tissue hair, brass split-pin
   joints, white paper eyes with a lid piece lowered over each. Stiff pinned movement is deadpan in itself.
4. **Ink line + wash** (`inkwash.png`): loose brush line with gaps and overshoots, watercolour laid off
   the line, pushed caricature (jowls, tiny close-set eyes, eye bags). Reads like a newspaper cartoon.
5. **Cross-stitch sampler** (`stitch.png`, own idea): 108 x 192 stitches on black cloth. Pious sincerity
   in a homely sampler; the chunky grid limits detail.

## Recommendation: 3. Paper cut-out
Chosen because it is the furthest from FilmCow while keeping the deadpan. The heavy lid piece makes the
sincerity funny without any acting. Cut-out figures are made to be animated cheaply: each piece swings on
its pin. Pieces can be reused across parodies too, so a new target only needs a new head, hair and props.
