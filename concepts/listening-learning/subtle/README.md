# Listening & Learning: subtle redesigns (keeping the original look)

The stage, spotlight, camera, pose, palette and flat style are exactly as in `source/ed.py`. Only the
character design changes. Made by `source/ed_variants.py` (the `stills` and `sheet` modes).

- **A. Coloured outlines:** lines in a darker shade of each shape's colour instead of uniform black.
- **B. Heavy lids:** rounder eyes with a blue iris, half covered by a heavy skin-coloured lid, plus a
  hard-edged shadow instead of the airbrushed one.
- **C. Rounder build:** pear-shaped head with fuller cheeks, shorter thicker neck, sloping shoulders,
  tapered arms, a fist with a thumb round the mic.
- **D. Grain and rim light:** a faint print grain on the figure, and a cranberry rim light on his shadow side.
- **E. All of A-D together (recommended):** the eyes and the construction are what make the film read as
  FilmCow, so changing only one of them isn't enough. Together they stay close to the original but are
  clearly our own.
