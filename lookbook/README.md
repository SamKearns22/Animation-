# Look book

Approved frames: the reference for how *Yuletide* looks. Every new shot is compared against these for
consistency (line weight, shading, paper, where colour appears, how the characters look). Add a frame here
only once the director has approved the shot it comes from.

| Frame | What it shows | What to match |
|---|---|---|
| `s1-mother-at-the-island.jpg` | Shot 1, first frame: the mother over the ham, cleaver raised | The set, the light, graphite everywhere except food, plants and decorations |
| `s1-mother-smiles-at-her-daughter.jpg` | Shot 1: she looks up at her daughter and smiles | Her face and a warm, genuine smile |
| `s2-daughter-from-her-mothers-eyes.jpg` | Shot 2: the girl across the island, from her mother's eyes | The girl (straw hair, red felt hat, sky-blue top), the radiator, the tartlets |
| `s3-close-up-smile.jpg` | Shot 3, first frame: close on her smiling face | The close-up: soft paper, fine hair strokes, the roll neck |

Rules (see `guides/tone.md`): muted graphite overall; coloured pencil only where it means something (food,
plants, decorations, the girl's hat and top, later blood).
