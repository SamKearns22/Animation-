# Sharing "Beaver vs Asteroid" on TikTok

A step-by-step plan for a first-time TikTok user, done entirely on an iPhone.
TikTok changes its menus from time to time, so a button may be worded slightly differently.

## Before you post

1. **Install TikTok** from the App Store and sign up. Pick a username that can grow into a series,
   for example `@unbotheredbeaver`. A character name is easier to remember than your own name.
2. **Keep it a Personal account.** Do not switch to a Business account: business accounts lose
   access to many sounds and features, and you do not need anything they offer.
3. **Set up your profile.** Use a still of the beaver's close-up face as your profile photo.
   Bio example: *"A beaver who does not care. Hand-drawn in coloured pencil."*
4. **Spend 15–20 minutes scrolling** animation and funny-animal videos. Like and follow a few.
   This teaches TikTok what kind of account you are, and shows you what people in that space post.

## The video itself

5. **Use the full-quality version** (`beaver-vs-asteroid.mp4`), not the Discord one.
   It is already the right shape for TikTok (tall, 9:16).
6. **Add a hook to the first seconds.** This is the most important step. TikTok viewers decide in
   1–2 seconds whether to keep watching, and our film opens with a calm, quiet forest. A line of
   text on screen fixes this. Recommended:
   **"An asteroid is about to destroy Earth. This beaver is busy."**
   Claude can burn this text into a special TikTok version of the video, so you do not need to
   use TikTok's editor. Just ask.
7. **Keep the original sound.** Do not add music. The silence on the beaver's face is the joke.

## Posting

8. Tap **+** at the bottom of TikTok, then **Upload**, and pick the video from your Photos.
9. **Caption:** short, with a question to invite comments, then 3–5 hashtags. For example:
   *"He had one job. Actually he had lunch. 🦫☄️ Would you have saved Earth?
   #animation #beaver #asteroid #funnyanimals #2danimation"*
10. **Cover:** tap **Edit cover** and choose the close-up of the beaver's face in white fire.
    That frame is what people see on your profile.
11. **AI label:** the film was made with an AI's help. Turn on TikTok's
    **"AI-generated content"** label (under "More options" when posting). It is honest,
    TikTok asks for it, and it protects your account from being penalised later.
12. **Post in the evening** (roughly 6–10pm your time). It helps a little, not a lot.

## After posting

13. **Stay around for the first hour** and reply to every comment. Early replies tell TikTok
    people are engaging, and that pushes the video to more people.
14. **Pin your favourite comment**, or pin your own, such as *"Part 2?"*
15. **Never delete and re-post** a video that did badly. Post something new instead.
16. **Never buy views or followers.** TikTok detects it and quietly stops showing your videos.

## Getting in front of more people

17. **Post the same video on Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts** (both accept 45 seconds, tall).
    Same hook, same caption. Three platforms means three chances to catch on.
18. **Share it in communities that love this kind of thing:** Reddit's r/animation and
    r/beavers, and your Discord servers.
19. **Make it a series.** This is the biggest lever of all. One video can get lucky; a
    recurring character builds followers. "The Unbothered Beaver" could stop a volcano,
    ignore an alien invasion, sleep through a tsunami... Claude can make the next one.
20. **Check what worked:** tap your profile, then the menu, then **Analytics**.
    The number that matters most is **average watch time** and how many people watched to the end.

## Realistic expectations

Most first videos get a few hundred views while TikTok tests them on a small audience. If people
watch to the end and comment, TikTok shows it to more. Going viral is luck; posting regularly with
a strong hook is how you give luck more chances.
