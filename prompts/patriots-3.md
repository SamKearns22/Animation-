1. THE IDEA
D-day. We watch from high above the coast, the black cliffs in the distance flashing with striating machine gun fire, artillery thundering down into the sands and spray, as six landers, stuffed to the brim with Allied troops, creep closer to the shore. It is a thunderous howl of gunfire.

We cut to inside a German bunker. A gunner fires his machine gun, the spotter directs him.

"Small boat problem?"

The men turn around, startled.

We see the Patriot, framed in the bunker doorway, leaning nonchalantly. "What can I say? Foreign invaders coming across the sea? Happens everywhere these days."

The soldiers look at each other and back to him. "Who are you?"

"Just a concerned citizen," says the Patriot, approaching. "Just a regular man with regular concerns."

The spotter says, "Ah, you are with the Furhër?" Both soldiers perform the Nazi salute. "Heil!"

"Jesus Christ, lads. The Free Speech Police are everywhere. Save that s*** (shit) for Whatsapp." The Patriot is visibly upset and waving his arm to quiet them, his arms darting around.

An explosion hits the lip of the bunker and the soldiers stumble back.

"Anyway," continues the Patriot, "got problems with the Establishment? People trampling on your sovereignty? Let's just say I'm here to assist."

The camera positioned in the slit and facing into the bunker, the Patriot mans the gun. He shakes his head sadly.

"They ain't even wearing poppies."

He begins to fire. The bunker is lit by muzzle flare.

We zoom back in on the soldiers standing dumbfounded in the background. Their eyes meet in confusion.
