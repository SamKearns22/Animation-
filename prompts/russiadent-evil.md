## The joke
Russian officials will deny a disaster however extreme it gets (Chernobyl is the classic example). Sticking to the
official line matters more than the obvious truth. Peskov calmly says all is well; behind the camera, a full
zombie outbreak is tearing through his own press conference, and the Ministry of Health's website still says
"Everything fine."
- **Play it completely straight.** Peskov never reacts: no flicker, no glance, no sweat. The deadpan is the joke.
- **The target is the official denial,** never the woman who died. She is never shown, named or referred to as a
  joke. The script says only "the researcher in question".
- **What would ruin it:**
  - Peskov reacting to the chaos;
  - gore so realistic that it stops being funny or gets the video restricted;
  - copying Resident Evil itself (its logos, the Umbrella symbol, its font, its music, its characters).

## The format
- **TikTok, native 1080 x 1920, as short as possible:** about 20 seconds (see the word budget below).
- Everything that matters stays inside the safe area (x 60-900, y 310-1500).
- **The opening:** the first frame moves (the slow zoom has begun) and Peskov speaks almost at once. The title
  **RUSSIADENT EVIL** in our standard cranberry treatment, gone by about 1 second, over the backdrop and never
  over his face. It is also the cover frame.
- **The hook is a person and a situation** (the DLC lesson): a man at a podium, talking, from frame one.
- **First laugh by about 8-10 seconds:** the 180-degree reveal of the outbreak.
- **The ending:** a hard cut to black straight after "Any more questions?", with picture and sound cutting
  together.

## The look
- Our TikTok parody look: flat shapes, clean black outlines, almond eyes with small pupils, soft shading,
  deadpan staging, hard cuts (Satire style, FilmCow-influenced, but our own: no dot eyes or FilmCow construction).
- **Peskov** as a recognisable caricature of a public figure (affectionate-to-sharp, never a traced likeness):
  - his big grey moustache, grey hair swept back, heavy-lidded calm eyes, dark suit, white shirt, tie;
  - at a podium with a microphone, in front of a plain blue official backdrop;
  - compare him with the series' existing caricatures so he doesn't resemble anyone else.
- **Real organisations are suggested through colour and generic signs, never their logos:** no Kremlin or
  government emblems, no real news outlets on mics or press badges, no Rospotrebnadzor or ministry logo on the
  phone. Generic press lanyards and microphone flags.
- **The zombies evoke Resident Evil without copying it:** grey-green skin, torn clothes, shambling and lunging,
  milky eyes. Some are former reporters (lanyards still on), which makes the spread readable.
- **Gore:** cartoon gore in the flat style: flat red splashes, no realistic anatomy, quick and comic rather than
  lingering. Keep it within TikTok's rules for cartoon violence.
- **Everyone has legs and feet**, and every background person has a clear action (fleeing, fighting off a zombie,
  hiding under a chair, filming on a phone).

## The location: one plan (best-practice.md, 1.3)
A Russian government press room. Write one plan: the podium and backdrop at one end, rows of chairs facing it, the
doors at the back and sides, tall windows down one wall, camera tripods at the back. Shot 2 is the 180-degree
reverse of shot 1, so both must come from the same plan.

## Script and shots (as I wrote it)
**Shot one: Peskov, slow zoom in (about 0-7 s).** We do not yet see the reporters.
> PESKOV: The researcher in question died from a common pneumonia. All is well. We ask anyone concerned to pay
> attention to bulletins from the Ministry of Health.

**Shot two: the 180 to the hall (about 7-10 s).** Pandemonium: screaming, chaos, zombies ripping through the
mostly Russian press corps. People bitten, mauled and tackled; blood flying; chairs knocked over; the doors and
windows blocked by tides of zombies. No way out.

**Shot three: the reporter (about 10-12.5 s).** A Russian reporter with a blonde ponytail crawls through the chaos,
white-faced and blood-splattered. She dives for her phone. Zoom in on her wide-eyed face as she types frantically.

**Shot four: her phone (about 12.5-15.5 s).** The Ministry of Health's website shows an official report:
**"Everything fine. Stop asking questions."** A zombie roars, then a crunch, and blood bursts across the screen.

**Shot five: back to Peskov (about 15.5-19 s).** Official and blank-faced. The screaming continues. A severed arm
flies up and hits the wall behind him.
> PESKOV: Any more questions?

Hard cut to black.
