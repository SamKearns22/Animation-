RUSSIADENT EVIL

STYLE
- Vertical TikTok. Satire style throughout. Cranberry Title style for the title, held a beat longer than usual. Hard cut to black at the very end.
- Hands are always simple circles. Props sit over them.
- Show me the cast and one still of each shot first. Then make one final version.

THE STORY
A Kremlin spokesman, Peskov, stands at a podium giving a calm, bored denial about a death at a plague lab. He says the researcher died of ordinary pneumonia and that everything is fine. He is lying, and the audience knows it.

Halfway through, we spin round behind him and see what he can't, or won't, see: the press room has been overrun by zombies. The reporters fight, flee and scream. A lone journalist crawls for her life, stops, and holds up her phone to read the official statement: "Everything fine. Stop asking questions." A zombie bites her, and blood sprays across the screen.

Then we're back on Peskov, who has not moved or reacted. A severed arm still holding a microphone slaps the wall behind him. He asks, "Any more questions?" Hard cut to black.

The joke is his deadpan. He never flinches and never looks round.

THE PLACE
A drab government press room: pale walls, a low stage, a blue backdrop with a flag and no emblem, and a plain wooden podium. Rows of blue chairs face the stage. Tripods and cameras sit on risers at the back. There are three tall windows on the left wall and doors at the back and sides. The room is shown from both directions, and it must look like the same room every time.

THE PEOPLE
- Peskov: grey swept hair, grey moustache, dark suit, red tie. He blinks slowly. (Reference photo attached; don't store it.)
- Nine reporters, all clearly different: body shapes, faces, hair, glasses, moustaches, jacket colours. They look Russian, not American. Each has a different kind of terror. No two are dressed alike.
- The heroine: a blonde ponytailed journalist in a pale jumper, with a press badge. She crawls on hands and knees.
- Zombies: men and women in office clothes, each damaged differently: a missing arm, a hanging jaw, a missing eye, a torn scalp, a gash. Cartoon gore only, flat red, no anatomy.

THE SHOTS (hard cuts; "slow" and "fast" are the pace)
1. Peskov, front on, medium shot, a slow creep towards him. Calm and still. Partway through, a bloodied hand slaps the front of the podium from below, clings, slides down and leaves a proper hand smear (palm, four finger streaks, drips). He ignores it. The smear stays on the podium for the rest of the film. His mouth moves with every phrase.
2. The 180: a fast cut to the other side, a wide shot looking down the hall from beside the podium. The room is in chaos.
   - Front left: a man with a chair watches a zombie come round him, winds up slowly, then brings the chair down on its head in one fast, hard blow. The chair buckles. The zombie flies backwards and lands flat on its back, with a bounce and a twitch. He stands over it, panting.
   - Front right: a man is held from behind by a zombie biting his neck. His eyes are on his attacker and his free hand shoves its face away.
   - Mid-hall: another attack, placed where this shot and the crawl shot both see it.
   - The window nearest the back cracks and bursts inward in a fast spray of glass. One zombie tumbles over the sill into a heap and drags itself up. Another climbs in after it with a knee on the sill. The window stays broken for the rest of the film.
   - Zombies pour through the back and side doors. Handprints appear on the other windows only where a zombie's own hand touches the glass.
3. A low, side-on shot of the heroine crawling away past the chaos. Everyone behind her is busy and moving.
4. A tracking close-up of her face as she keeps crawling. She stops and lifts her phone in one circle hand to her eyeline. Her eyes dart about, then lock on the screen. Its cold light is on her face. Slow and tense.
5. Insert of the phone screen: a Russian Ministry of Health page with the English line "Everything fine. Stop asking questions." held in the same hand, no typing. A roar behind her, a crunch, and blood sprays across the screen and her hand, only onto the things it hits. The phone slips from her grip and falls out of frame.
6. Back on Peskov, same pose, a slow beat of calm. The severed arm with the microphone flies in and slaps the backdrop behind him, leaving a red splat with a drip. He doesn't react. After a short pause he says his last line. Hard cut to black.

THE VOICE (my recording; clean up the outdoor noise and shorten the long pauses, but don't cut any words)
- "The researcher in question died from a common pneumonia. All is well. We ask anyone concerned to pay attention to the bulletins from the Ministry of Health."
- "Any more questions?"

SUBTITLES (these exact chunks, no more than two rows)

- died from a common pneumonia.
- All is well.
- We ask anyone concerned
- to pay attention to the bulletins from
- the Ministry of Health.
- Any more questions?

SOUND
- Screams, roars, crunch, splats and the slap are all made in code.
- The crowd noise sits low under the voice, even lower under the last line. The last line is clearly louder than the rest of the hall.
- Voice and picture cut to black together.

KEEP TRUE THROUGHOUT
- Anything that changes stays changed: the podium smear, the broken window, the knocked-over chairs.
- Anyone seen in a close-up must also be visible in the wide shots.
- In every fight, each person looks at whoever they're fighting.
- No frozen background people; everyone is doing something.
- Never show, name or joke about the dead researcher. No copying Resident Evil. No real logos.

BEFORE THE FINAL
- Run all your checks, and show me the subtitles as a list to proofread.

DELIVER
- Send the video here with a notification. Tell me the file size and how long it took. Update the project status and save to GitHub.
