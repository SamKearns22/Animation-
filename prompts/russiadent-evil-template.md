```
FILM: RUSSIADENT EVIL. TikTok parody, FilmCow style: flat colour, thick black outlines, circle hands, deadpan. No gradients, no wobble or grain.

FORMAT
- 1080x1920, 12 fps, about 28.6 s, MP4 under 5 MB. Save as animations/russiadent-evil-vertical.mp4.
- Title RUSSIADENT / EVIL on two lines, cranberry (178,24,52), on screen 2 s.
- Hard cut to black after the last line.
- Process: cast sheet and one stills sheet for my approval, then ONE final render. No animatic unless I ask.

THE JOKE
- Peskov denies a plague-lab death in a deadpan voice while a zombie outbreak tears through the press room behind the camera. He never reacts or looks round.
- Never show, name or joke about the dead researcher. No Resident Evil copying. No real logos.

REFERENCES (attached)
- Peskov photo (do not store in the repo).
- South Park frames (circle hands, props laid over them).

CAST (cast sheet first, I approve before animating)
- Peskov: grey swept hair, moustache, dark suit, red tie, slow blinks.
- Nine reporters, all different: builds, faces, hair, glasses and moustaches, jacket colours, Russian look. Different kinds of terror (scream, wail, grimace, gasp). No two outfits alike.
- Heroine: blonde ponytail, pale jumper.
- Zombies: both sexes, varied damage (missing arm, hanging jaw, missing eye, torn scalp, gash). Flat cartoon gore only.
- Hands: plain circles. Props are laid over them.

SET AND CAMERA
- One press-room plan, perspective cameras. Three left windows, back doors, side doors, risers with tripods, podium on a low stage, blue backdrop and flag, no emblem.
- Shot 1: front, medium, slow push-in, 0-13.8 s. Peskov at the podium.
- Shot 2: reverse wide from beside the podium (the 180), 13.8-16.8 s.
- Shot 3: low side view of the heroine crawling, 16.8-18.3 s.
- Shot 4: tracking close-up of her face, 18.3-20.8 s.
- Shot 5: insert of her phone screen, 20.8-24.2 s.
- Shot 6: back to Peskov, 24.2 s to the cut to black.
- All cuts are hard.

ACTION BEATS
- 5.2 s: a bloodied hand slaps the podium's front edge, clings, slides down and leaves a hand smear (palm print, four finger streaks, drips). The smear stays whenever the podium is visible.
- 14.4 s: the right-hand window cracks, then bursts in with shards. One zombie topples over the sill into a heap and drags itself up. A second climbs in with a knee on the sill. The window stays broken after that.
- Handprints on glass only where a zombie's own hand touches it.
- Chair man (front left): watches the zombie come round him, winds the chair up, ONE hard hit at 15.3 s, the chair buckles. The zombie flies back and lands flat on its back (thud, bounce, twitch). He stands over it, panting.
- Grabbed man (front right): zombie behind him bites his neck. His eyes are on the attacker and his free hand shoves its face.
- Mid-hall attack in clear view in shots 2 and 3, placed where both shots see it.
- Heroine crawls away past the chaos, stops, lifts the phone in one circle hand to her eyeline. Her eyes dart, then lock on the screen. Its light is on her face.
- Shot 5: the phone is held in the same hand, no typing. Zombie roar, a crunch, blood spray lands only on the phone and hand. The phone drops out of frame.
- Shot 6: a severed arm holding a microphone slaps the backdrop (red splat, drip) behind him. He does not react.
- Eyelines: everyone in a fight looks at the person they fight.

VOICE (my recording, cleaned of outdoor noise, pauses shortened, no words cut)
- Line 1: "The researcher in question died from a common pneumonia. All is well. We ask anyone concerned to pay attention to the bulletins from the Ministry of Health."
- Line 2: "Any more questions?" (after a short beat in shot 6).
- Lip-sync across every spoken phrase. No missing mouth movement.

SUBTITLES (exact chunks, 2 rows maximum)
- The researcher in question
- died from a common pneumonia.
- All is well.
- We ask anyone concerned
- to pay attention to the bulletins from
- the Ministry of Health.
- Any more questions?

SOUND
- Crowd, screams and roars are made in code. Hall bed 20% quieter than my first version. Bed ducks further under line 2, and line 2 is 20% louder.
- Master to -14 LUFS, peaks under -2 dBTP. Sound and picture cut to black together.

CONTINUITY
- Keep a list of permanent changes with start times: podium smear (from 5.2 s), broken window (from 14.4 s), knocked chairs. Every shot draws from it.
- Anyone in a close-up must appear in a wide shot.

CHECKS (must pass before the final)
- Pre-flight: arms, overlaps, unique outfits, neck join, eyelines, mouth coverage, safe area, title 2 s.
- Print the subtitle list with times for me to proofread.

DELIVER
- Send the video in the chat and a push notification. Tell me the render time and file size.
- Update story/status.md. Commit and push to the branch.
```
