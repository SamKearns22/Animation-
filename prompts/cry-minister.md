# CRY MINISTER (Sam's brief, 8 Oct 2026)

Satire-style TikTok film. Follow `guides/satire.md` and start from `source/satire_template.py`. Andy is the Hope Again
model from `source/burnham.py`.

## STYLE
- Vertical TikTok. Satire style throughout. Cranberry Title style for the title, held a beat longer than usual. Hard
  cut to black at the very end.
- Hands are always simple circles. Props sit over them.
- Show Sam the cast and one still of each shot first. Then make one final version.

## THE STORY
Andy Burnham is known as the TikTok Prime Minister. He gives sincere, energetic promises and addresses to the nation
on the platform. Here he is filming a TikTok in his usual style, the camera framing him differently between points.
He is making a political point about wolf attacks on our high streets. He explains the problem, sets out the little he
can do about it, then breaks down a little at being unable to afford anything, and at the end he is chased away by
wolves, still in TikTok selfie mode.

## THE PLACE
- The audience sees the action as if watching a real TikTok post on Andy Burnham's official feed (Sam to send
  references).
- As he talks, the picture cuts back and forth: mid shots, closer for the points that hit hardest, and wider shots when
  he needs to show something out of view.
- He stands on a British high street. Directly behind him, so not always seen: a traditional Tudor pub, The Goose and
  Cranberry; a betting shop, ZapBets; and a dilapidated, boarded-up empty shop.

## THE PEOPLE
- **Andy Burnham:** the Hope Again model. Picture references from Sam show the level of stress and pressure.
- **The man:** a Man City football shirt, black tracksuit bottoms, white trainers, short light brown hair.
- **The old lady:** little and petite; a pink frock (a black-trimmed dress); curly white hair; a twitching right eye; a
  far-away expression; a walking stick; large white-and-pink striped glasses; a maroon handbag.
- **Wolves:** large, snarling, ferocious, more like Game of Thrones dire wolves than anything ever native to Britain.
  Fast and agile, with hungry, manic yellow eyes and oversized serrated teeth. They lope and bound. To attack, they
  fling themselves through the air with snarling abandon at their prey's throat. When they feed, they rip hunks of
  meat up towards the sky and cover themselves in blood.

## THE SCRIPT (each line is one shot; hard cuts)
1. Mid shot. Andy sincere, gently opening his arms as if making a good point in a debate.
   "Now here's a familiar sight you must recognise."
2. Mid shot, but we see only half of him. In the left half of the screen, a pack of five wolves launch themselves onto
   the man and rip him to bloody shreds. Andy carries on without a pause.
   "Wolf attacks on British high streets. They're violent, they're concerning, and they've got to stop."
3. Back to the first framing. Gentle gesturing.
   "My Labour government are doing everything in their power to get this under control."
4. A large wolf snarling. It suddenly changes into a wolf 60% of the size.
   "We've implemented smaller wolves."
5. A wolf chases a man down an alleyway, runs into a net and gets tangled up in it.
   "More wolf nets down every alleyway."
6. Andy stands beside the old lady, gesturing at her with his thumb, with a sly, satisfied expression. As he speaks,
   Kevlar armour suddenly appears on her, with a wolf biting uselessly into it. She doesn't seem to mind.
   "And means-tested wolfproof armour for pensioners."
7. Close-up. Sincere and concerned.
   "But there's more to do."
8. Mid shot. Pleading.
   "So I'm asking you, begging you."
9. Back to the close-up.
   "Please get a job."
10. Mid shot. He spreads his arms wide, imploring.
    "Any job."
11. A little further away.
    "We have NO money."
12. A comically wide shot that takes in the whole high street, the wolves still feasting on the man.
    "You want change when I can barely afford to faff about with the margins."
13. Far, far too close: just his eyes, red-rimmed.
    "I'm so stressed."
14. Mid shot. Real desperation, a slightly crazed look round the edges of his eyes.
    "I haven't slept for weeks."
15. Close-up. A face-palm of exasperation.
    "I CAN'T stop every wolf attack."
16. Mid shot. A football "COME ON, SON" movement.
    "I know it's a problem,"
17. Close-up on the wolves mauling the man.
    "Okay,"
18. Desperate mid shot.
    "Just STOP YELLING."
19. A sincere, normal close-up.
    "I'm going to keep fighting for a fairer and safer country."
20. He runs away. Selfie-style, filming over his own shoulder: his face on the right, a bit of his shoulder; the wolves
    chase him, gaining.
    "Aw fook, here come some now."

## Questions for Sam before starting (each with a recommended answer)
1. **Voice:** will you record Andy's 20 lines, as for Hope Again? *Recommended: yes, one take per line, recorded indoors
   so there is no wind.*
2. **References still to come:** the TikTok post look you mentioned and the stress pictures for Andy. *Recommended:
   send both before the cast sheet.*
3. **The "watching his TikTok" look:** when posted, the real TikTok buttons and caption sit on top of the film, so a
   full fake TikTok screen would show two sets of buttons. *Recommended: suggest it lightly. Handheld selfie framing
   with a slight wobble, a small parody handle (for example @TheTikTokPM, never a real logo or his real account name)
   and one short on-screen caption of his, all inside the safe area. No fake like, comment or share buttons.*
4. **Gore:** TikTok can stop showing films with strong gore to new viewers. *Recommended: cartoon blood in flat red, as
   in Russiadent Evil. The man is hidden under the pack and we see meat flung up rather than open wounds.*
5. **Wolf count:** five in the attack. How many chase him at the end? *Recommended: the same five, so it pays off.*
6. **Length:** the script is about 45-50 seconds of speech, so the film will run about a minute with the action.
   Advice only, it is your call: our best films so far are 15-30 s, but a film over 1 minute will count for Creator
   Rewards later.
7. **Shot 4 (smaller wolves):** an instant "pop" down to 60% (funnier), or a quick shrink? *Recommended: an instant pop.*
8. **Title:** CRY MINISTER, on two lines, the Cranberry Title held for 2.5 s instead of 2. *Recommended: yes.*
