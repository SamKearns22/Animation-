# The New Press Secretary (working title) – brief (draft, waiting on Sam's answers)

```
THE NEW PRESS SECRETARY

STYLE
- Vertical TikTok, about 25 seconds. Satire style throughout. Cranberry Title style for the title. Hard cut to black at the end.
- Hands are always simple circles. Props (the note cards) sit over them.
- Show me the cast sheet and one still sheet first.

THE STORY
The new White House press secretary gives her first briefing. She reads from her notes in halting, near-gibberish
sentences, stopping in odd places and stressing the wrong words. Every few seconds she looks down at her notes, then
looks back up at the room with a huge, strange, too-long smile. The camera creeps slowly in on her the whole time.
It ends on her looking down one last time, a big flat sigh, and "...Venezuela." Hard cut to black.
The joke is the delivery: she never once sounds like she understands what she is reading.

THE PLACE
The White House press briefing room, from Sam's reference photos: a dark wooden podium with a black front panel
("THE WHITE HOUSE / WASHINGTON", red line between), a blue backdrop with the oval White House seal, a US flag on a pole
to her right (screen left), two thin microphones on the podium. In front of the stage, the backs of reporters' heads,
several with hands raised.

THE PEOPLE
- The press secretary (a cartoon, never named on screen): very long wavy blonde hair past the shoulders, heavy
  make-up, navy double-breasted blazer with gold buttons, white blouse with a tie-neck and keyhole. Note cards on
  the podium. Her look: a big wide toothy smile she switches on and off; a numbered strength sheet to pick from.
- Reporters (wide shot only): backs of heads, all different hair and jacket colours, some hands up.

THE SHOTS
1. One continuous shot. Starts wide from the back of the room (reporters' heads and raised hands in the foreground,
   her at the podium, flag and seal behind). A slow, steady push-in for the whole film, ending on a close-up of her
   face and shoulders for the sigh and "Venezuela".

THE VOICE AND THE WORDS (draft; Sam records)
  Good morning. I am... so honoured. To be. Here.
  (looks down)
  President Trump has... amplified. And multiplied. And... expanded. The amplification.
  (big smile)
  Of wins. For... Americans. For... for Americans.
  (looks down)
  Attendance is at... record... highs. Higher. Than the... previous highs.
  (big smile)
  He is an energetic... president. Very... energetic.
  (looks down)
  A historic... one-day... war. A booming... economy. And a great relationship...
  (looks down, big flat sigh)
  ...with Venezuela.

SOUND
- Voice only, no sound effects.

KEEP TRUE
- Her notes stay on the podium. The reporters are always the same people.
```
