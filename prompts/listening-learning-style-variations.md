# Prompt: new looks for Listening & Learning (paste into a new chat)

Continue the animation project. First read CLAUDE.md, story/status.md and guides/tiktok-design.md, and
follow them (plain English for me; make the choices yourself; say how long renders will take and report
afterwards; a push notification when renders finish; send pictures in the chat).

**The problem:** "Listening & Learning" (source/ed.py, animations/listening-and-learning.mp4) borrows its
look very heavily from FilmCow's cartoons (flat shapes, uniform thick black outlines, dot eyes, a plain
static staging, a sincere deadpan voice). The borrowing is too blatant. I want a look that keeps the essence
but is clearly our own, for future topical parodies on the CranbriJoos account.

**Keep (the essence):**
- deadpan: a sincere character, a still camera, absurdity played completely straight;
- cheap and fast to make, since topical parody has to be out within 24-48 hours of the news;
- instantly readable on a phone, and working with burned-in captions and our cranberry-red title card;
- caricature that is recognisable without being a likeness copied from a photo.

**Change (so it is no longer a copy):** anything that is FilmCow's signature rather than just "simple
cartoon": the dot eyes, the uniform heavy outline, the flat untextured fills, the proportions and the
character construction. Do not imitate any other named artist's style either.

**What to do:**
1. Study ed.py and a few frames of the video. Write a short plain-English list of the visual traits it
   shares with FilmCow, and which of them we actually need.
2. Propose **five distinct directions**, each with a one-line reason it keeps the essence. Consider at
   least: a coloured-pencil version in the family of our other films ("Dam", "The Salt") so the account
   has one recognisable house style; a two- or three-colour printmaking look (lino or woodcut, or a
   risograph-style limited palette with grain); a paper cut-out look; a loose editorial-cartoon line with
   a flat colour wash; and one idea of your own. Vary eyes, line quality, texture, palette and
   proportions - not just the colours.
3. For each direction, make **one concept still** of the same moment (the "It is my belief" line at
   17.5 s: the pop star at the mic, sincere, spotlit), at 1080 x 1920 inside the TikTok safe area, with the
   caption and the colour kept honest to how it would really render. Put all five, plus the current look,
   on one comparison sheet and send it.
4. Recommend one, saying why, and how long a full re-render of a one-minute film in that style would take.
5. Stills only - do not re-render the film or change the posted video. Save the concept stills in
   `concepts/listening-learning/` and write the chosen direction into guides/tiktok-design.md once I pick
   one, so future parodies use it.
