# PREPPER MINISTER - Sam's brief, updated 24 Oct 2026

Satire-style TikTok film. Follow `guides/satire.md` and `guides/film-quality-rules.md`, and start from
`source/satire_template.py`. Plan only until Sam says go.

## 1. THE IDEA
Andy Burnham, the "TikTok Prime Minister", films a calm public-information post about stockpiling supplies. Step by step
it becomes clear he knows an AI robot invasion is imminent. He goes from beans to weapons, the robots break in, and he and
Wes Streeting fight them off inside the house. Shot 12 runs 7 seconds, then a hard cut to black.
Background (not in the film): this week Andy Burnham and Wes Streeting told the UK to stockpile food and water in case of
war, climate chaos or an AI catastrophe. As with Cry Minister, we are watching Andy's real TikTok post.

## 2. TONE AND LIMITS
- Shock level 3. Nothing fully cartoony; it carries a certain gravity.
- Weapons have verisimilitude (in our style). The crossbow fires with a meaty thump.
- The robots' attack gets convincing effects: anything struck or destroyed gives believable debris, sparks, flames,
  whatever the moment calls for. Drawn in our flat house style: bold, hard-edged shapes that last a few frames, no glows.
- Sweat is wanted here: small flat beads, not cartoony.
- The Alexa may be a real Alexa. No other real logos.

## 3. THE WORLD
A presentable upper-middle-class London home, refined yet contemporary. Living room and kitchen connect naturally (open
through a wide opening). A door in the back wall of the living room leads to a well-lit entrance hallway; a second door
joins the hallway to the kitchen.
- Living room: large modern TV opposite the sofa (the camera of shot 1 stands where the TV is), drinks cabinet to the left
  of the hallway door, a watercolour of the Lake District hills to the right of it, a plush old-world armchair, forest
  green carpet, a grand window.
- Kitchen: kitchen island, modern amenities, slate-effect worktops, a grand window at the back (robots climb through the
  windows).
Claude draws a simple top-down sketch of this layout for Sam to approve (no metres needed from Sam), and works out where
every door, window and camera stands.

## 4. THE CAST
- **Andy Burnham:** smart casual, white shirt with the top button undone. The Hope Again model. Mood moves from friendly
  and collected to hysterical and terrified; Claude sets a stress level per shot.
- **Wes Streeting:** a full politician's suit, ripped and torn as though he has just escaped danger.
- **The robots:** gleaming chrome Cylon-style warriors (from the 2004 Battlestar Galactica; Sam sends references and
  Claude designs from them). A single piercing cobalt eye runs horizontally back and forth like a searchlight. Some have a
  glowing cobalt blade for one arm; some have an arm cannon that fires blue plasma. They are numbered 1 to 5 in shot 12.
- **The Alexa:** a real Alexa speaker. After shot 6 its battered remains return in shot 12 on tiny metal rod legs.
- **Props:** crossbow, minigun with a loop of ammo, pistol, oily machete, tins of beans, 2-litre bottles of water.

## 5. THE SCRIPT, SHOT BY SHOT
Each shot picks up exactly as the last one left things, except shots 5 and 6, which are a cutaway to an earlier moment
(Andy preparing in the kitchen), not a sequential part of the film. Hard cuts unless stated.

**Shot 1.** CAMERA: mid shot, Andy in the middle of the sofa, leaning forward a bit to look into the lens. ON SCREEN: Andy,
living room, door to the hallway behind him, drinks cabinet left of the door, Lake District watercolour right of it.
ACTION: a small flick of a wave with his right hand. LOOKS: into the lens. WORDS: "Hello, Prime Minister here."
EFFECTS: the TikTokPM frame from the final Cry Minister on every frame of the film (name and description only).

**Shot 2.** CAMERA: as shot 1 but a bit closer. ACTION: gesturing with his right hand in a circle, left elbow casually on
his knee. WORDS: "So, no biggie."

**Shot 3.** CAMERA: extreme close-up. ON SCREEN: Andy's face, stress lines under the eyes, a grin halfway to rictus.
ACTION: slight trembling. WORDS: "But you need to stockpile, now."

**Shot 4.** CAMERA: as shot 1. ON SCREEN: Andy with more rigid anxiety in the face and tiny beads of sweat. ACTION:
imploring at the lens, serious urging. WORDS: "I mean, like, right now."

**Shot 5 (cutaway).** CAMERA: mid shot of the kitchen island, no one in shot; Andy's voice carries on over it. ACTION:
items appear on the island on the beat of each word: a few tins of beans, 2L bottles of water, a crossbow, a minigun with a
loop of ammo. All detailed and immediately recognisable. WORDS: "I don't just mean beans and water. Crossbow. Minigun."

**Shot 6 (cutaway).** CAMERA: mid shot, Andy waist up in the kitchen; when he fires, the camera follows the bolt across the
kitchen to the Alexa on the worktop. ACTION: aiming down the crossbow sights; on the second line he fires; the bolt strikes
the Alexa and it shatters into fragments. WORDS: "Test them. Test them on your Alexa." EFFECTS: metal fragments and
components flying; the crossbow fires with a meaty thump; the whoosh of the bolt; sparks as the machine bursts.

**Shot 7.** CAMERA: as shot 2, back on the sofa (no crossbow in shot). ON SCREEN: Andy, pained, imploring, much sweat.
ACTION: urging desperately. WORDS: "I can't stress how little time you have."

**Shot 8 (one moving shot).** CAMERA: starts as shot 7. It sweeps forward past Andy's shoulder and curves round to look back
at the hallway door. Andy is now in the room opposite the door, just behind the camera, crossbow in hand. ACTION: the door is
booted open. Wes Streeting enters under great duress, holding an oil-covered machete. WORDS (Wes): "It's too late. They're
here." A gleaming robot appears over his shoulder, its cobalt eye sweeping back and forth. A crossbow bolt streaks through
the camera position, fired by Andy whom we never see, and hits the robot's head dead centre. It falls back. EFFECTS: blue
sparks from the wound as it crumples.

**Shot 9.** CAMERA: wide shot of the living room from the same position as shot 1. ON SCREEN: Andy has his back to us,
aiming the crossbow at the door; Wes shuffles into the room away from the sparking robot. ACTION: Andy spins to the lens,
chucks the crossbow, and whips a pistol from behind his belt. He is pale and shocked.
WORDS: "We are already out of time. Did you get the beans? DID YOU GET THE BEANS?"
ACTION 2: a robot lunges from the left of the screen, a glowing cobalt blade for an arm. Andy sidesteps neatly and blasts a
few holes into it, dropping it.
WORDS: "Streeting, you pussy, there's a minigun in the kitchen." The word "pussy" is bleeped; the subtitle reads "p****".
EFFECTS: sweeping blue arc from the blade, pistol blasts, whipping bullets, metal fragments and sparks.

**Shot 10.** CAMERA: mid shot. ON SCREEN: the minigun on the kitchen island, the window behind, kitchen storage and counter
to the right. ACTION: Wes runs up (dropping the machete), drags the minigun into his hands and throws the ammo belt over his
head like a bandana. Robots appear at the window behind him. He snarls (a facial expression only, no sound). A burst of blue
plasma from an arm cannon shoots into the kitchen past him. He turns and blasts the robots with the minigun. EFFECTS: glass
shattering everywhere, thick streaking plasma bolt, minigun muzzle flashes, bullets, sparks.

**Shot 11.** CAMERA: close-up on a can of beans on the island, then following it. ACTION: Andy's hand grabs the can, pulls
back and launches it across the room, where it dents harmlessly against a robot coming through the hallway-to-kitchen door.

**Shot 12 (7 seconds, then hard cut to black).** CAMERA: one 360-degree loop around Wes and Andy, who are pale and
freaking out on either side of the kitchen island. ACTION:
- Wes fires the minigun manically at robots 1 and 2.
- Andy dodges plasma bursts from robots 3 and 4 and fires his pistol at them.
- Robots 1 and 2 walk in from the lounge, blade arms extended.
- Robot 3 approaches Andy firing plasma; Andy shoots its head off.
- Robot 4 crawls in over the back window sill.
- Robot 5 fires plasma into the room from the hallway door and misses.
- The damaged Alexa climbs back to its feet on tiny metal rod limbs, holding up a sharp kitchen knife, and scuttles towards Wes.
- Whenever Andy or Wes finishes an action they take aim at a new target fluidly and organically. Wes never stops firing
  whichever way he shoots, his eyes follow his targets, and anything he shoots breaks and shatters.
EFFECTS: the room flashing with minigun fire; plasma, blade and pistol effects; wood, ceramic and tiling erupting where struck.

## 6. CONTINUITY
Kept by the action itself: everything starts where the last shot left it (apart from the shot 5 and 6 cutaway), and anything
broken, dropped or killed stays that way for the rest of the film.

## 7. VOICE, CAPTIONS AND SOUND
- Sam records every voice: Andy's lines (shots 1 to 7 and 9) and Wes's one line (shot 8). Wes's snarl in shot 10 is a facial
  expression only.
- Sound effects: Claude gives Sam the list of references (see the list below, adjusted if the shots change), and Sam finds
  them on a free sound-effect library.
- The bleep in shot 9 is one of them. The word is gone after the bleep; "p****" appears only in the subtitle.
- Hook Text (the line introducing the joke) comes later from Sam, with the CranbriJoos sign-off.

## 8. THE SCREEN FRAME
The TikTokPM frame from the final Cry Minister, on every frame.

## 9. BEFORE ANYTHING IS BUILT OR SHOWN
1. Claude lists the photo references still needed before drawing anything, and Sam supplies them. The list:
   - Wes Streeting: straight-on, three-quarter and talking photos, plus one or two caricatures Sam likes.
   - The Cylon robot (2004 Battlestar Galactica): front, side and three-quarter views, a close-up of the eye, and both arm
     types (blade and cannon).
   - The living room and the kitchen: modern upper-middle-class London rooms like those described, from several angles,
     including empty and from above.
   - The props: a modern crossbow, a minigun with its ammo belt, the pistol Sam wants, a machete, an Alexa speaker.
   - Claude says which are still missing, and never draws from memory.
2. Claude asks all remaining questions in one batch, each with a recommended answer.
3. Because the film is ambitious, Claude first renders short rehearsal clips of the hardest moments: the 360-degree loop
   in shot 12 and the crossbow-bolt tracking move in shot 6.
4. Then the house sketch, the cast sheet, one still per shot and the animatic, as the film rules say.
