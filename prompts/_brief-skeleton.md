# Brief skeleton (copy, fill in; see prompts/russiadent-evil-template.md for a finished example)

TITLE / STYLE: Filmcow style. Cranberry Title style. Ending: hard cut to black.
THE STORY: (3-5 sentences: who, what happens, the joke, what the main character never does.)
THE PLACE: (rooms, objects, left/right/back details; shown from which directions.)
THE PEOPLE: (each: look, build, clothes, how they act. Say who must differ from whom. Cast sheet first.)
THE SHOTS: (each: camera position and angle, size, "slow" or "fast", what happens, who looks at whom.)
THE VOICE AND THE WORDS: (exact lines; my recording per line; clean outdoor noise; shorten pauses, never words.)
SUBTITLES: (exact chunks, two rows at most.)
SOUND: (what is made in code; how loud the hall is against the voice; the last line louder/quieter.)
KEEP TRUE: (what changes and must stay changed; who is seen where; what must never be shown.)
BEFORE THE FINAL: (checks; proofread the subtitle list.)
DELIVER: (send here with a notification; size and time; update status; save to GitHub.)
