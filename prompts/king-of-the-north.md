## The idea
A parody of the Labour Party conference that slowly and absurdly turns into the famous "King in the North"
scene from Game of Thrones.

## The look
The same visual style as our "Listening & Learning" video (source/ed.py): flat shapes, clean black
outlines, dot eyes, deadpan staging, a still camera with hard cuts. Reuse its drawing code and helpers where
you can, so it matches. Make it natively vertical: 1080 x 1920, following guides/tiktok-design.md (safe
area, cranberry-red title card, burned-in captions inside the safe area).

## The scene, shot by shot
1. **The stage.** Andy Burnham (a caricature, recognisable but simple, in the same style as our pop star)
   at the conference lectern, delivering the line (my recording):
   *"The British Right talk about 'taking back control'. Never let them forget, they are the ones that
   gave it away in the first place."*
2. **The hall.** Cut to the conference hall on its feet in a standing ovation. The ministers who were at
   the conference sit in the front row, caricatured.
3. **Knight one.** Mid-shot of an attendee: an elderly white man in the northern knight's armour and furs
   from the "King in the North" scene. He yells: *"King in the North!"* Around him, the hall keeps clapping.
4. **Knight two.** Cut to another: a middle-aged Sikh man, same armour. *"King in the North!"*
5. **Knight three.** Cut to another: a young blonde woman, same armour. *"King in the North!"*
6. **Back to the stage.** Burnham is now dressed as Jon Snow in that scene (dark cloak with a fur mantle).
   He stoically soaks up the adoration, looking slowly left, then right. Deadpan.
7. **The hall again.** Now everyone is in northern costume, waving swords. The clapping and whooping swell.

**The joke:** the conference turns, slowly and absurdly, into the famous scene. Keep every beat deadpan
and played straight: the escalation is the joke. The first knight should land as a surprise in an
otherwise ordinary conference.

## Things to handle carefully
- **Evoke, don't copy:** costumes, armour, the hall and the sound should clearly evoke the scene without
  copying the show's actual imagery, logos or music. No Game of Thrones theme music; use crowd sound
  (clapping, whooping, cheering, sword clatter) that we make or that is free to use.
- **Real people:** Burnham and the ministers are caricatures for satire, never realistic likenesses and
  never traced from photos. Keep them affectionate-to-sharp, not cruel.
- **Party branding:** suggest the conference with colour, a stage and a generic backdrop slogan rather
  than copying the party's real logo exactly.
- Captions for every spoken line; the shouts can be big caption text too if it helps the rhythm.
