## The idea
A TV street interview at the Patriot Platform protest outside Dover. A masked protester says he is stopping
an invasion; the reporter points out that he is actually blocking British people and British border officers
on the British side. He answers like a child in a playground. The joke: the protest is badly thought out and
a bit stupid - and his own arm badge says so.

## The look
The same style as "Hope Again" (`source/burnham.py`, `source/burnham_film.py`): flat shapes, clean black
outlines, almond eyes with small pupils, soft shading, deadpan staging, a still camera with hard cuts.
Reuse its drawing code (people, clothes, gesturing hands, `gesture_hand`, keyed arm poses, captions,
title) and its film code (timeline, sound, mouths that follow the recording) so it matches. Natively
vertical: 1080 x 1920, inside the safe area (x 60-900, y 310-1500).

## Where
A road on the UK side of the border controls at the Port of Dover, grey and windswept: a queue of cars and
lorries stopped behind the protest, crash barriers, a generic "Border Control" sign and booths in the
distance (no real port or Border Force logos), the white cliffs faintly behind, gulls. A few more masked
protesters loiter in the background with St George's flags. Somewhere behind them, a Border Force officer
in a hi-vis vest and a family with suitcases are stuck, unable to get through.

## The people
- **The protester:** anonymous - not a caricature of any real person. Black balaclava, only his eyes
  showing; black jacket and combat trousers. A round sew-on patch on each upper arm, both visible to the
  camera (he faces it, three-quarter on):
  - **left arm:** "PP" in bold capitals, like a badge of the group;
  - **right arm:** a crude, felt-tip, schoolboy doodle of a cock and balls weeing a stream (the badge is
    the second "PP": pee pee). A doodle, never anatomical; it should look like he drew it himself.
  - **He cannot move a mouth, so he talks with his body:** every line is acted with his arms, head and
    shoulders, timed to my recording (bigger gestures on the loud words), and his eyes do the rest.
- **The reporter:** a fictional middle-aged woman - not a caricature of any real journalist. Tall,
  short auburn hair, smart coat, a microphone with a plain mic flag (a made-up channel, or none). Calm,
  professional, deadpan; her mouth follows her recording.

## The scene, shot by shot
1. **Two-shot.** The reporter holds the mic out to him. Protester (*boorish*, puffing up, thumb jerking at
   the sea): *"We're here to stop an invasion on our borders, love!"*
2. **Reporter.** Single shot, calm: *"And you're doing that by blocking citizens from accessing the UK side
   of the border and preventing UK immigration officers from doing their jobs?"* Behind her, the stuck
   Border Force officer and the family with suitcases.
3. **Two-shot.** Protester (*extremely childish voice*), mimicking her: head wobbling, prissy little hand
   flaps, pretending to hold a mic: *"Oooooh, I'm a reporter, I think through what I do and say."* Then
   (*accusing*), jabbing a finger at her: *"You're a traitor, love!"* Hold a beat on her unmoved face and
   the badges, then a hard cut to black.

**The joke:** he is stopping an invasion by stopping his own country's border, and his answer to a fair
question is a playground taunt. Play it completely straight: the reporter never reacts, the camera never
comments. The pee-pee badge is the visual punchline - visible all along, but noticed late.

## Things to handle carefully
- **Real group, fictional people:** Patriot Platform and the Dover blockade are real (September 2026).
  The protester and the reporter are invented; never base either on a real person or trace a photo.
- **Captions:** every line, word for word as I record it (I may change words when recording - follow the
  recording, not this script). The childish line can be captioned in a sing-song style if it helps.
- **The doodle badge:** keep it a small, crude cartoon. TikTok may limit or remove videos showing genitals,
  even as cartoons - check the current rules before posting and keep a version with the badge blurred.
- **Sound:** my voices exactly as recorded (volume only - no echo, no pitch change). Wind, gulls, idling
  engines and a distant horn, made from scratch. No music unless I ask.
