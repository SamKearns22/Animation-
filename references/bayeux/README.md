## Director's rule for every swap (5 Oct 2026)
- **Delete the original weapon completely** (bow, arrow, bowstring, spear, axe head and shaft): no trace of it is
  left. Where it crossed the linen, stitch plain linen back; where it crossed the soldier, his horse or his shield,
  restitch what would be behind it in the same stitches and colours.
- Draw the new weapon **in the same place** as the original, in the soldier's same hand(s), sized and angled to his
  grip, **in the same embroidered style** (stem-stitch outlines, laid-and-couched fills, the tapestry's wools).
- The soldier, his horse and everything else in the scene stay exactly as in the reference.
