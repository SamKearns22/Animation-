# Milburn: the shot board's plan rows (rule 11). One block per shot.

shot 1
camera: level at seated eye height (1.2 m), straight across the Cabinet table, lens 1700; Milburn 2.2 m away
lead: Milburn, centre, straight to lens
focus: his face, under the Hook Text band
next focus: the four faces at the bottom of 2a (eyes drop from his face to theirs)
match: his voice runs on over the cut (line 2 starts in 2a)
choices: empty chair each side; window and green curtain right; red folder, glass and his name card on the baize

shot 2a
camera: level at standing head height (1.6 m), lens 2000; every head on one line (F2); medium shot to the torso, room above
lead: the goth (nearest, left); the others at 3.6, 4.8 and 6.2 m
focus: the four faces; then the light sweeping down the wall to them ("training")
next focus: the shaft's mouth at the middle of 2b
match: the platform rising (wall slides down) carries into the platform rising into the field
choices: wall lit warm from above as the light comes down; they go from shaded to lit; caged lamps slide past

shot 2b
camera: high and tilted down 33 deg (45 m up), lens 1000; peaks, forest ring and meadow as bands of the tall frame (F1)
lead: the platform at the centre, the four tiny on it (F8)
focus: the deck coming up and locking level with the grass ("country")
next focus: (the next part of the screenplay, not yet written)
match: same four, same colours, tiny
choices: grass gusts in the wind; snow on the peaks; lots of empty ground on purpose

shot 3
camera: level, standing head height 1.6 m, mid shot, four plinths at 3.0-7.0 m, staggered
lead: the goth front left with the crossbow; the rangy dead centre; Swindon back right; the waif back left
focus: each face and what each holds (pleased, inspecting, gung-ho, terrified)
next focus: Andy's face in the reverse
match: Milburn's voice runs on over the cut; same four, same colours
choices: four plinths only; weapons lifted off their plinths

shot 4a
camera: level 1.2 m from Milburn's seat, lens 1450-1500; Andy and Pat 2.2 m away, equal and centred
lead: Andy, left of centre, in the PM's chair
focus: Andy's face and jabbing finger
next focus: Milburn, shot 1's camera
match: the same chairs, depth and camera height as Milburn's shot
choices: Pat on his left, eyes only to Andy; fireplace behind, no portrait

shot 4b
camera: shot 1's camera: level 1.2 m straight across the table, lens 1700
lead: Milburn, centre
focus: his face, nonplussed and soft
next focus: the reverse two-shot
match: same camera as shot 1
choices: glass and name card as shot 1

shot 4c
camera: level 1.2 m from Milburn's seat, lens 1450-1500; Andy and Pat 2.2 m away, equal and centred
lead: Pat, then Andy folding his arms
focus: Pat's mouth, then Andy leaning back
next focus: Milburn's face
match: same as 4a
choices: Pat steepled hands; Andy sulking

shot 6
camera: level 1.4 m, side on, travelling right with them at walking pace, four depth layers; eases to a stop on the gunfire
lead: the four in single file: goth, Swindon, rangy, waif
focus: their weapons and faces, then the flame and morning stars from the right
next focus: Andy exploding in the reverse
match: Milburn's voice over; the attackers never seen
choices: ferns race past, far trunks crawl; goth fires a bolt into the air
