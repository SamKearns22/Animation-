# Open Wounds: the shot board's plan rows (rule 11). One block per shot. Built and shown in film order (Sam, 24 Nov).

shot 1
camera: level, 1.15 m up, lens 1450 (wider than the 1800 plan: note 9, May about waist up); May 1.6 m away, the slatted wall 3 m behind
lead: May, seated, body and face turned to screen-left, looking off-frame left at the unseen interviewer (item 14)
focus: her face, centre right (about 62% across), eyes about 36% down, the Hook Text band clear above her head
next focus: Kemi's face in the same place and size (Sam, 24 Nov: the interview look)
match: the same face place, eye row and head size in all three interviews
choices: dark slatted wall with soft warm lamp streaks, as her photos; her chair seen at the right behind her (the tall frame shows her lap); no background action

shot 2
camera: level at her eye height (1.58 m), lens 1100, Kemi 1.21 m away; the concrete frame moved back to 26 m so the grey sky shows over it (item 16)
lead: Kemi, standing, turned to screen-left, in May's place and size (same head top, eye row and chin), staring off-frame left
focus: her face; then the wolves' action around her (attack low left, milling left, bar fight and runner above)
next focus: Blair's face in the same place
match: the interview look (face place, eye row, head size)
choices: daylight under a grey British sky; dust, blood as the approved plan

shot 3
camera: level, 1.2 m up, lens 1700, Blair 2.06 m away; the double doors 7 m behind, left of his head
lead: Blair, seated in the armchair, turned to screen-left, in May's place and size
focus: his face and grin; the doors thrown open behind; the lunge from frame right
next focus: the sign-off
match: the interview look
choices: dark oak, books, chandelier, red rug; the cut on the lunge
