# Animation test: four seconds of the trailer, ending on the THUD

(The director's outline, word for word, then the notes we work from.)

> Shot 1: this shot. She chops two slices of ham, her eyes noticing her daughter across the kitchen island.
> She smiles.
>
> Shot 2: the daughter, a straw hair waif with curious eyes, in a droopy felt Santa hat, staring at her over
> the island with a blank, mildly curious expression, watching her cut.
>
> Shot 3: a close up of the mother's smiling face. The camera tracks down her body until we see her gripping
> the ham. The THUD noise begins as she slices towards her outstretched fingers with a cleaver chop.
> Important: it fades instantly to black before impact, leaving us with darkness and the last moments of the
> noise.

## Working notes
- Total length about 4 seconds at 24 frames a second, drawn on twos (12 new drawings a second), fast action
  (the chops) on ones.
- Shot 1 starts from the approved still (cleaver raised over the ham, left hand pressing it steady).
  Two chops, each: lift (anticipation), fast fall, impact jolt, settle. Her eyes find her daughter
  (a saccade, then the head follows), then the smile builds slowly (about 0.5-1 s).
- Shot 2 needs a second character (the daughter) built on MakeHuman like the mother, with straw-coloured
  hair and a droopy felt Santa hat (the hat in red coloured pencil, as a decoration). Blank, mildly curious.
- Shot 3: the camera starts close on her smiling face and tracks down her body to her hands on the ham.
  The chop falls towards her own outstretched fingers; cut to black *before* the blade lands. Sound carries
  on over the black.
- The cut to black is instant (not a slow fade) - it lands on the first frame of the THUD.

## Director's answers (decisions)
- **Length:** six seconds for this first test: Shot 1 = 2 s, Shot 2 = 1.5 s, Shot 3 = 2.5 s.
- **The daughter:** on the opposite side of the island from her mother, off screen in Shot 1, almost
  opposite her and slightly to the mother's left. About 6-7 years old, eyes just over the worktop.
- **Shot 1 chops:** two neat slices, about 1 cm thick, fall away onto the board next to the first slice.
- **The smile (Shots 1 and 3):** warm and genuine - cheeks and eyes involved. The horror is how normal it is.
- **Shot 2:** from the mother's point of view - camera near her eye height, looking down across the island at
  the girl's face and droopy felt Santa hat just over the worktop edge; ham and board soft in the foreground.
- **Shot 3:** she looks at her daughter, smiling, the whole time - never down at the blade.
- **Sound:** use the trailer's own audio. Before the cut we hear only the Deck the Halls refrain and the
  sounds of her cutting the earlier slices. The section of the trailer audio where the THUD sounds is placed
  so the THUD lands exactly on the cut to black.
