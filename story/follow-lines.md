# Follow lines (Sam, 12 Nov)

The follow line on each film's sign-off (rule 52). `finish.py` takes the next unticked line the first time a film is
finished (its animatic) and ticks it with the film's name; the same film always gets the same line after that. When
none are left, `finish.py` stops and asks Sam for a new batch (it warns when 2 are left). Compare followers per 1,000
views film by film in `story/metrics.md`.
