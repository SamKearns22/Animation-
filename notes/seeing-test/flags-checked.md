## What this says
- About a third of the watch judges' flags on passed moves are real faults Sam let pass (a floating gun, a hand
  with no arm, bystanders not reacting to an explosion). About 40% are misreadings, mostly something covered by
  another thing in front, which the judges read as a vanish.
- So the watch sheets are a real gain, not noise, but they need a second look at full size before anything goes
  to Sam; on their own they would cost rounds chasing misreadings.
- New pattern for the checks: "vanished" flags are most often a cover-up by something in front. The who-is-where
  copy (way 1) knows the drawing order and can settle exactly those, so the two belong together.
