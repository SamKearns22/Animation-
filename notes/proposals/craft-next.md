# Proposal: The next craft change after the census: which one, and how

Status: open

## Problem
Sam said Y (16 Nov) to a craft census: every craft note from the last three films sorted by area (sets, effects,
framing, cameras, people), cause, repeat and fix cost, so that the next changes come from the evidence, one at a time.
Two changes are already made (crowds varied by default; poses checked at phone size in their own shot). The census is
done. The decision is which ONE craft change to put to Sam next, and in what form, so that the first build of a shot
needs fewer of Sam's craft notes on the next films, every film new, without trimming the joke or reusing content.

## Facts
- The census: notes/proposals/craft-census.md (ranked lists per area and across areas) and
  notes/proposals/craft-census/notes.tsv (every note, its tags). 159 craft notes: people 80, framing 24, effects 22,
  sets 20, cameras 13. Causes as tagged: tool default 54, missing check 45, taste 31, no composition 29. 45 repeats.
  Tagging is one chat's reading: counts exact, cause and cost are judgments (the census says so).
- Already covered (counted in the census, not open): crowd sameness 12 notes (change 1, ON); poses at phone size 14
  notes (change 2, Y); mouths/house face 5 and likeness 3 (done earlier, rule 49 and the mouth check).
- Biggest open groups by notes saved (census, "Across all areas"): composition of each shot before building 19 notes
  (EPR 9, Blessed 6, Remakes 4; weight 36); contact and eyeline (hands reach, feet down, eyes on whom they address) 11;
  camera height and distance 8 (weight 13); sets and kit against reference photos 8; effects tied to what makes them 8;
  nobody in shot still 7 (4 repeats); effects that live over time 6; frame placement (focus centred, no empty
  half-frame, clear of the app's buttons) 5; continuity across cuts 4; group moves in step 4 (all repeats); effect
  sizes 4; clipping through sets 4.
- Sam (16 Nov): EPR was built with no composition in mind, so its framing notes partly record his own late choices.
  Blessed and Remakes had scripts and still gave 10 composition/framing notes between them.
- Sam declined (16 Nov) a moving rough before building and a note-sorting chat (notes/proposals/epr-lessons.md); its
  judge found the France and Albania cameras passed still checks and pencil roughs and failed only when seen moving.
- Framing lessons F1-F12 (research/crowds-and-big-action.md; tall frame in bands, eye level first, focus near the
  centre at cuts, etc.) were written as proposals on 12 Nov; rule 10 (guides/film-quality-rules.md) says framing is
  planned up front but its only check is eyelines and holds. Whether any of F1-F12 are in the rulebook: rule 10 and
  rule 89 mention framing (the helpers must check).
- The plan Sam said Y to (notes/proposals/craft-first-round.md, Final version, item 3) named likely next yeses: effect
  sizes checked in metres against the nearest person; framing lessons F1, F2, F10 into rule 10 with a check.
- Numbers to beat already set: change 1 at most 2 sameness notes on the next two crowd films; change 2 no pose note
  returned twice on the next two films.
