# Answered faults come back when the drawing changes (Sam asked, 26 Nov, chat 36)
Problem: a "this is fine" note is matched to one person, one second and exact words. When a later round changes the drawing, the same kind of fault appears on someone else or at another second, has no note, and nobody has looked at it; old notes are never rechecked.
Steps: 1 each note is saved with a picture of the frame it was judged on, and the drawing's fingerprint at that moment. 2 when the drawing in that spot changes, the note is marked "needs a fresh look" and shown as a circled still beside the old one. 3 a note can cover a kind of fault for a named person across a time range, not one second. 4 the animatic reports each reopened note in one line; it never blocks (rule 93).
Why: faults answered once stay answered only while they look the same.
You'd notice: a short "Reopened:" list with stills on animatics, fewer surprise faults.
Other option: leave notes as they are and only list how many are older than the last drawing change.
Lost: nothing. Added: a picture and fingerprint per note, a "Reopened:" line.
