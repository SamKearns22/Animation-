# The chat's own idea (written before the helpers)

1. Sam's pictures become a labelled test set: each picture of his, cropped to the fault, with his words and the
   fault's kind (limb shape, contact, unexplained shape, see-through, clipping). The critic is shown three of these
   beside every new frame pack ("this is what Sam calls broken; find anything like it").
2. A full-size "joints and contacts" sheet in every critique pack: for every frame, crops at full size of each
   shoulder, elbow, hand and every place a body meets the car or the ground. The critic judges these crops, not a
   phone-size panel.
3. New measures in the body check for what Sam named: a drawn limb's length against its thickness (sausage, stub), a
   hand's distance from what it rests on, two forearms crossing, an unlabelled dark bar crossing a body.
4. A camera test before animating: each planned camera, with the poses, scored for where the flat drawing breaks
   (an arm end-on to the lens, a body seen from close behind); a camera that breaks it is moved before anything is
   drawn.
Why: Sam's notes repeat because our checks look at bones and order, not at the picture. The pictures he has already
pointed at are the cheapest teacher we have.
