# The chat's own idea (written before the helpers ran)

Shot plan before building: framing lessons F1, F2 and F10 written into rule 10 with a check. In the plan, every shot
names its camera height and distance chosen for what it must read as (F2: eye level first), its focus point inside the
safe area (F10), and its layers stacked in bands of the tall frame (F1). Pre-flight refuses a shot without them; on the
stills, a check measures that the named focus sits near the centre of the safe area, that no band of more than about a
third of the frame is empty, and that nothing key sits under the app's buttons.
Would have saved: camera height/distance 8 notes, frame placement 5, part of composition 19 (the "empty space",
"too far", "too close" ones). Cost about 0.5-1% of a week once. Number to beat: at most 2 framing or camera notes on
the next two films, none repeated.
Lost: nothing in the films. Added: one planning line per shot and one check.
Other option: the contact and eyeline check (11 notes): hands meet what they hold or hit, feet on the ground, eyes on
whom they address, measured from the drawn hand points every frame.
