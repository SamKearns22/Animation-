# The chat's own idea (written before the helpers ran)

Tried first (chat 3): May and Kemi with the existing `build woman`, arms 27, legs 0.92. Almost nothing changes at
full height: the waist the figure carves sits behind the hanging arms, the hips end at the same 0.85 hem, the jacket
is the same boxy cut, the trousers and shoes are the man's. So the existing figure alone does not answer Sam.

1. A "woman" body in the shared kit, one line (`build woman`), that changes what shows at phone size, not only the
   hidden waist: shoulders narrower and sloped (rounded, not square corners), the arms hang a little away from the
   body so the waist shows between arm and jacket, the jacket fitted in at the waist and flaring out over the hips
   (hips wider than the waist, about the shoulders' width), shorter jacket. Every view (front, three-quarter, side,
   back) through the existing build outline (body.FIGS and views._built_outline), so turned views keep the shape.
2. Legs: slimmer trousers that taper to the ankle; women's shoes: a small court shoe (a low heel and pointed toe)
   or flats; `shoes court|flat|boot` (Kemi: work boots, as her site clothes).
3. A woman's jacket cut for the suit: smaller lapels set higher, no man's notch; May's plum jacket gets it.
4. Crowds: every crowd woman (people.crowd_person) gets the woman build by default, so every later film has it.
5. A check: pre-flight / castsheet refuses a cast person marked as a woman (or with women's hair, lashes) drawn on
   the man's build, and measures waist-to-hip and shoulder slope on the drawn silhouette against a set range, tested
   on Sam's saved n5 pictures (they must fail) and Lucia (must pass).
6. Show Sam a cast sheet v3 of May and Kemi on the new build before any shot is built.

Cost: about one chat (Opus, medium) for the build and check; risk: old films with women (Lucia, Milburn's women)
change if the figure changes: keep the old figure under its name for them (freeze recut checks).
