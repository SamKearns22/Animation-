## Leak
None that matters ("one change at a time" is the route's own rule).

## Results

- **The film grew 2.4x:** planned 16 s + sign-off (plan-r0 line 153), finished 43 s. Sam added length at animatic 1 (R4 4/6/8/10 s; R2 a 7.6 s end scene) and at animatic 2 (G1, A2 "2-3 s longer"). Cost per finished second went from about 1.2% to 2.4%.
- **About 104 notes after animatic 2** (O, S, G, F, A series; my count). Many were new direction, not faults. Cameras were reversed on 3 of 4 tiers: F1 France "too low, a mess" (planned 0.8 m, plan-r0 line 64), A1 Albania "perspective flattens" (planned 3 m, line 72), G4 Germany.
- **Those cameras had passed two still checks:** the framing research (10.08%, unplanned) and the pencil roughs Sam saw (rule 11; handovers lines 33-37).
- **6 repeat notes (rule 30):** F15/O4, F16/F9, F18/F2, F20/F6, A30/A12, plus G4, a miss of the rule 83 hand check. One chat's work was lost unpushed and redone (Germany chat 6 to 6b).
- **Gap:** the ledger records 0 notes and 0 repeats for every chat, so the cost per kind of note cannot be read. Chats 9-32 cannot be opened.

## The real decision
The real question is when a film's length, cameras and pacing are fixed, for every film. Notes that arrive after building multiply the cost.

## Root cause
Sam judges staging and length only on **moving pictures with the full crowd and effects in them**. Everything before was still. So the cast, sets and moves were built at the wrong length and from the wrong cameras, then rebuilt city by city on clips (rounds 3-5).

Why the earlier fixes failed:
- The rule 11 roughs show where things go, not how dense the scene reads in motion.
- The rule 74 acting list is words.
- The estimates priced the screenplay's 4 s tiers.

## Approaches weighed
1. **Learn-first:** sort every EPR note as new direction, added scope, fault or repeat, cost each kind, and make the ledger count notes.
2. **Reframe: a moving blocking pass before building.** A blocking pass is a rough moving version at the real cameras and real length with existing crowd sprites (ready-made cut-out figures) and boxes for new people. Sam gives his Y before cast starts.
3. **Per-second pricing:** each added second gets its own Y.: prices growth, tempts cuts.
4. **Automatic repeat and clipping checks** (rules 30 and 83).
5. **A push guard against lost chat work.**

My choice: **1, then 2.** It targets the 67% spent after the animatic and cuts no joke. 4 and 5 come later, each on its own evidence.

## Proposal
**Learn (changes nothing, about 1.5%, Sonnet):**
- Sort the EPR notes into the four kinds and give each kind its share of the round costs.
- Fill in the ledger's notes and repeat counts.

**Change 1, only if the learn step shows at least 30% of the round cost came from length or camera notes:**
- A blocking-pass stage in every film, after the plan and before cast.
- **Enforced:** the ledger's stage order and the handover check refuse to start cast without Sam's Y on the blocking clip.
- **Cost:** about 1.5-3% per film (unchecked), against the 15% target.
- **Number to beat:** the share of cost spent after the first animatic (EPR: 66%) must fall below 35% on the next two films, with at most 2 length or camera notes. Reviewed when Sam declares each film complete.

**Sam would notice:** one early clip of grey figures moving at full length. His "higher, longer" notes land there, and later rounds get smaller.
