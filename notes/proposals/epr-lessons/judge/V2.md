# The chat's own idea (written before the helpers ran)

Root cause guess: EPR cost about 5x its first plan because it was priced as an ordinary film while being new in three
ways at once (crowd technique, research, speculative script). The stage budgets assumed a plan that the research then
changed. The biggest overrun is round 2 (about 47% of a week against 0.9 planned): notes arriving on a finished-looking
animatic and being fixed one city at a time across many chats.

1. A read-only lessons step first: from the ledger and notes, set every overrun against its cause (research,
   re-staging, notes on animatic 2, rework, handover and cost-watch overhead), per stage, for the whole film; name which
   causes belong to this film's kind and which would hit any film.
2. Then separate changes, one at a time, each with a number to beat:
   (a) a "kind of film" flag at plan time (new technique, research, speculative script) that sets a larger, stage-gated
   estimate and requires a script lock (a beat list Sam confirms) before cast and sets are built;
   (b) a small proof-of-technique step (one tier, a rough) before the full cast build, so a new crowd technique is judged
   before four tiers of work stand on it;
   (c) bring Sam's first round of notes earlier, on a cheap rough rather than on animatic 2.
3. Review each against the film's results at the end-of-film review.
Cost: the lessons step about 1% of a week; each change 0.5-1.5%. Undo: each is a single rule or check line.
