# The chat's own idea (written before the helpers)

Option B, front-on focus as the house default for every person, plus a "best route" guard, plus a retirement list.

1. Build one "focus" kit piece on every front head (house head, people-kit front head, people_famous): a single number
   `focus` (-1 left to +1 right) that moves whole pieces, not pixels: both pupils into the corner, the eyes, nose and
   mouth slid a few per cent toward the focus inside the unchanged outline, the nose tip turned, the near brow lower
   and the far brow higher, the mouth's corner pushed, a head tilt of up to 6 degrees, and an optional 10-15 degree
   shallow turn drawn by the front head itself (the outline's far side narrowed slightly), never by the 3D head.
   Glasses, beards, hair and piercings keep their front design (BoJack's rule). Tested on Andy and Kemi first, sheet
   to Sam, then on one invented person.
2. Rule 38's check reads the DRAWN angle on every film (not only films without a rooms plan): a person drawn past 25
   degrees without a THREE_QUARTER_OK reason stops the animatic. The moves kit gets `focus` as a move (eyes lead,
   head tilt follows, a gesture last), and the acting list gets "focus, not turn" for listeners.
3. The real turned views (side for walking, behind) stay for walks and exits only, on whichever head is best.
4. Invented people: give the house head a "pick from a description" (no photos: the people-file lines turned into a
   pick's numbers), and move the people kit's cuts and parts onto it, so there is one front head for everybody.
   Bigger job: priced separately, its own Y.
5. A "best route" table at the top of the kit catalogue, one row per stage (people, turned people, places, props,
   effects, moves, sound, finishing, checks): the best route first, every older route marked "kept because X" or
   "retire". A check (pre-flight) fails when a new film reaches a route marked retire or skips the row's best one.
6. Retirement list, each its own numbered line with Lost/Added; archive (recoverable, under source/archive/) rather
   than delete; the three-films rule checked against the ledger: the 3D MakeHuman stack, hands3d, duel, the labs and
   one-offs, `head: measured` (once Blessed's frames are re-keyed or kept), the long-red kittests fixed or retired.
   Finished films that still render from per-film files keep them.

Lost: the true three-quarter look in new films (only by a THREE_QUARTER_OK reason); some build time (focus piece about
1.5% of a week, description-pick about 3%, guard and table about 1%). Added: front-quality detail in every shot, one
head for everyone, and no film choosing a weaker tool by accident.
