## What the board would need to show more
1. A brief-cast check on each row (who the brief puts in the shot versus who is drawn). Walliams' absence would then show.
2. Camera height and direction marked on each row (eye-level/high/low; facing which way), so missing reverses and camera heights (11, 56, 61) stand out.
3. Measured flags on each frame: subject height as a share of the frame, how far the subject is off centre, empty-area share, and anything outside the red dashes.
4. Durations of key actions (how long a firing or hold lasts) and a check for pop-ins between frames, since three stills miss timing faults (47, 17a).
5. A continuity strip for props that repeat across shots (banner, castle, door position) side by side, to catch 75, 61 and 46-type jumps.
