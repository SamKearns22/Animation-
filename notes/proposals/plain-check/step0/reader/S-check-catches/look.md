## Retelling
- Option 1, step 1: A short chat for each of three films tests the current checks against how those films were made, producing one table of what each check found, wrongly flagged, how long it took, and which of your notes it should have spotted.
- Option 1, step 2: Then, each only if you say yes, make the checks automatically keep a record of their results, and label each of your past notes with the check that should have caught it.
- Option 2, step 1: One chat reads the old notes and records of the three films and, by hand, sorts each check's results into real catches, false alarms and misses. Cheaper, but the records say little, so many answers would be "could not tell".
- Option 2, step 2: Same two fixes as option 1 (automatic record, note labels).
## What I'd answer
I would ask first what "the checks" are and what "a week" of budget means. Leaning to option 1 because it gives a firmer answer, if the cost is acceptable.
