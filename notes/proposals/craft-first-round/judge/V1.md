# The chat's own idea (written before the helpers ran)

Root cause guess: the craft lessons exist (framing research F1-F12, the pose gallery, rule 11 roughs) but sit in
research notes and in tools that check after building, so a first build is made from the brief and the faults are found
in rounds. What a first round lacks is a per-film "look plan" that fixes, before building, the things the faults came from.

1. First, a read-only audit (about 1% of a week): which of F1-F12 and the craft items are already rules or checks and which
   are only notes; count how many of EPR's notes each existing rule should have caught and did not. Nothing changes.
2. Then separate changes, one at a time, each with a number to beat:
   (a) a one-page look plan per film before cast and sets: for each shot the eye level, focus, safe-area bands and light;
   (b) a crowd variety setup in the cast stage: each city's mix of skin, build, clothing and hat colour set from a list,
       with a one-line automatic check that no colour or model exceeds a share;
   (c) a pose test at phone size from the shot's own camera angle, made part of the existing pose gallery step, so a
       hidden hand or small gesture shows before the scene is built;
   (d) the moving-camera check made earlier, using the existing rough command in motion.
3. Each reviewed against the film's results at the end-of-film review.
Cost: audit about 1%; each change 0.5-1.5%. Undo: each is one rule or check line.
