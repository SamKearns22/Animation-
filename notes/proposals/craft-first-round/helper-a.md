## The real decision
Which craft faults reach Sam across all films, and which a check could catch first: a bigger lever than written lessons.

## Root cause
The checks look at the wrong thing.
- Sameness: the copies check (`source/kitchecks.py` line 24) flags only two people identical in every detail. 70 of 147 gold hats, or too many bald heads, pass it. Crowd sprites missed the skin fix (F15). F9 varied only rate and phase, within 15% (F16).
- Effects: rule 18 sets only a smallest size. Nothing measures a stone, crater or fire in metres against a nearby person, so the sizes were wrong again after the camera moved (F20).
- Poses: the shrug was tested alone, never by eye, never in its shot with the launcher at phone size.
- Cameras: judged on stills. The moving fix was declined on 16 Nov and is not repeated here.

## Approaches weighed
1. Write the lessons into the rulebook. This depends on a chat reading and following them, which is how A12 and F9 failed.
2. Reframe: the faults came from having no fixed composition, so enforce rule 11's sheet. That sheet already exists and cannot catch sameness or size.
3. Shot-level checks on the finished frame at phone size: a mix check, a real-size check and a pose-in-shot check.
4. Learn first: sort the craft notes from EPR, Blessed and Remakes by class, cost and "which check would have caught it", then build checks in cost order.

Chosen: 4 leading into 3.

## Proposal
**Learn (changes nothing):** one Opus chat sorts and prices the craft notes of the three films. About 2% of a week, once (unchecked).
**Then one change at a time, each its own yes, in the order the learn step finds.** The likely first:
- **Mix check** (only if sameness is in the top two classes on two of the three films). It extends the copies check. In pre-flight, every crowd frame, sprites included, is refused when one hat, hair or colour passes a set share of heads, when skins miss the place's mix, or when neighbours move in step. Number to beat: at most 2 sameness notes per film with none repeated, on the next two crowd films (EPR had 16, 3 repeated). Build about 2-3% (unchecked).
- Later, separately: a real-size check (effects in metres against the nearest person, merged into rule 18) and a pose-in-shot check (each key pose's outline drawn in its shot at phone size, clear of the body and props).

Each is a pre-flight refusal, not reading, reviewed at film completion. Sam would notice fewer crowd and effect notes.
