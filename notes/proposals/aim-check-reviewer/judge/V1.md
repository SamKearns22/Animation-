## Leak
Small: the problem points at "choices made inside a stage" (F4) and the queued lessons, which hints at moving the reviewer to re-stages. Weighed below, not assumed.

## Results
- Kit misses: 3 (2 clear, 1 partial), all Milburn, about 0.1-0.2% of a week in all; 0 challenged (study.md). The reviewer costs about 1-1.5% a film and, by its own fault F6, pays only on about one Milburn-sized miss (aim-check.md). On this record it returns about a tenth of its cost.
- Milburn: estimate 24.0%, spent 21.99% (notes/ledger/milburn.md); Sam's app figure 22% (notes/archive/milburn.md line 1). **It landed under its own estimate: its whole 7-point overrun was set at the plan** (estimate already "over the 15% aim", archive/milburn.md lines 94, 109), mostly a doubled screenplay, which is content and is never cut.
- Blessed: estimate 19.0%, spent 48.63% (notes/ledger/blessed.md). **About 30 of its 34 points came after the plan**: rework of returning faults ~15% (Walliams' face 10.9%, repeats ~4%), chat 5's bundling ~4% avoidable, critique helpers ~a quarter of each build chat (notes/cost-diagnosis.md lines 40-47).
- Milburn's inside-stage waste: reverse staged three times, scene 3 re-staged, 3/4 fix 3.6% in one chat, search and re-reading 15-35% of most Opus chats, 4 repeat notes of 33 (notes/proposals/milburn-review.md lines 7, 45).
- Gap: no record splits each chat's spend by cause (first build, rework, re-stage, reading, checks). The ledger shows 0 notes and 0 repeats for both films (ledger headers), and Milburn's stages closed late (milburn-review.md line 29). So the size of re-stages, the cause the reviewer would move to, is unmeasured.

## The real decision
Not "how should the reviewer question choices" but "which lever, across a whole film, closes the gap to 15%". The record shows two different gaps: one set at the plan (Milburn) and one made after it by rework (Blessed). Both are bigger levers than kit reuse.

## Root cause
The reviewer was sized on a guess (missed cheaper ways), made before any cause-by-cause split existed. The guess failed because the plans did reuse the kit (study, "rows found sound"). The money went where no pass looks: faults that came back, re-stages, long chats, and reading. Underneath that, the ledger cannot count notes or repeats, so no chat could see these costs while it worked.

## Approaches weighed
1. **Build the reviewer as sized.** The record says it would recover about 0.1% a film for 1-1.5%. Rejected.
2. **Move the reviewer to re-stages and repeat notes (reframe).** It aims at F4's money, but its size is unmeasured, and queued lessons 3 (two framings before staging) and 1 ("could not test" stops the animatic) already target the same causes with Sam's Y. Building it now would put two changes on one cause.
3. **Learn first: a cause-by-cause cost table for both films**, then one change at the top cause. Chosen.
4. **Plan-time pricing against 15%**: when the estimate passes 15%, the plan names smarter tools for its dearest new rows (never cuts). This is real for Milburn, but Sam declined repeated cost messages (aim-check.md, 19 Nov 09:14), and it waits for the table.

## Proposal
**Sam's yes now (one):** Change 1 (the fresh reviewer) is closed unbuilt, because its own bar fails by about ten times. Nothing goes in CLAUDE.md or the rulebook.

**Learn (changes nothing; about 1% of a week; Sonnet):** split each chat's app cost in Milburn and Blessed (ledger figures, 366 USD a week) into: first build of planned rows, rework of a returning fault or repeat note, re-stage, reading and search, checks and critique, bundling excess. Fill both unchecked items. Did each re-stage's cost come from a note Sam had already given (in the chat records)? Would a framing pick have caught the reverse? Output: points per cause, plan-set gap against after-plan gap, and which causes the queued lessons 1, 3 and 8 already cover.

**Then, one change at a time, each with its own yes:**
- First, the queued lessons run as Sam ordered. Lesson 3's number to beat: no shot staged more than twice. Lesson 1's: no repeat of a fault a check could not test.
- Next, only if the table shows a cause over about 3 points a film that no queued lesson covers, one change aimed at it, through the route with that number. It is enforced by `handover.py end` (the tool), not by a chat's reading.
- Number for the whole film, checked at its end review: rework plus re-stages under 3% of a week (Blessed about 15%), and the film within 15% of a week, or within its estimate where Sam's brief alone sets it over.

Cost: about 1% now, against 1-1.5% a film for the reviewer. Sam would notice no new step. The next proposals arrive with their causes priced.
