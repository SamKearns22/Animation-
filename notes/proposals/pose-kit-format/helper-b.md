## The real decision
How every later new pose, in any film, starts on the proven route, catches seam faults before Sam, stops a dead method within a round, and books its true cost. A bigger lever than a document.

## Root cause
1. The proven route was never put where a kit chat starts, so it is relearned at 25-39%.
2. Nothing stops a failing method.
3. Seams are checked only after Sam names one, and only on May.
4. Cost goes to whichever ledger the chat named.

## Approaches weighed
1. **A written guide.** It relies on a chat choosing to read it, and it leaves causes 2 and 4.
2. **An enforced kit route.** The handover check refuses a kit chat that skips its steps.
3. **Reframe:** mode 'body' becomes every person's default (the solid-body-default chain), and a pose becomes joint targets in the film's own Build. This is bigger, but untested standing.
4. **Learn first:** probe the route on other people and outfits.
5. **Reframe on cost:** fix booking and stopping only.

Chosen: 4, then 2 one part at a time, plus 5's booking fix (already measured). The route becomes approach 3's Build step rather than a fork of it.

## Proposal
**Learn (changes nothing; Opus medium, about 2%).** Kemi (vest) and Blair, seated in mode 'body', each on a sheet beside their posted look. Every R7 check is run and each seam gets a viewer pass. The record shows which checks fire, miss or cannot run. No fixes.

**Change 1 (now, about 0.5%).** `handover.py start --film` books each chat to that film, and R7's entries are moved into r7.json. To beat: the kit ledger equals its chats' app costs.

**Change 2 (after Learn, about 2.5%).** A kit-job pack and a catalogue row for mode 'body'. The handover check refuses a kit chat unless:
- its pose is behind a flag with a switch test;
- a reference photo is saved;
- the sheet stands beside the posted look;
- each seam on the Learn list has a check.

To beat: the next pose kit costs at most 6% and gets no Sam note that a listed check covers.

**Change 3 (after Learn).** A method stop: if the judges' mean does not rise by a point in a round, the next step is a "what must be learned" question to Sam. It goes live only once the judges agree with Sam's verdict on mode 'body'.

Cost about 5-7%, one-off, against R7's 15% of waste.
