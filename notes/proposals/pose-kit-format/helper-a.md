## The real decision
How every future body or pose job reaches Sam's "correct" in two or three chats. Bigger levers than a written order: a true cost signal, a forced method change when judges stall, and layer-edge checks on every person before Sam looks. It belongs inside the solid-body-default chain.

## Root cause
Chats 1-5 guessed shapes and patched view by view ("each patch moves another view", seated-legs-3d.md); nothing forced a method change while scores stood still. 'Whole' replaced the posted look instead of keeping it. Once the method was right, faults lived where layers meet, unchecked before Sam. Misfiled ledger rows hid the overspend.

## Approaches weighed
1. **Written order of work** (a job pack): relies on a chat choosing to follow it (fails Q3).
2. **Reframe: the format is code.** A shared pose command makes the standard sheet (every view beside posted look and reference photo) and runs every layer-edge check; the send guard refuses a failed sheet.
3. **Learn first:** map every Sam note and dead end in all 11 chats to what would have caught it.
4. **Reframe wider:** no per-pose kits; mode 'body' is the default (solid-body-default) and a pose is joint targets plus a reference.
5. **Stall stop:** two judged rounds without a whole-point rise stops patching and requires a proven method from the catalogue.

Chosen: 3 first, then 2, 5 and the ledger fix as separate changes, inside 4's chain.

## Proposal
**Learn (changes nothing; about 0.7%):** the 11 transcripts (list_events, filtered) into one table: note or dead end, layer, catching step or check, cost. Output: ordered steps with evidence.
**Then one change at a time, each with its number:**
- **C1 ledger (0.3%):** a kit job's chats go in its own ledger; handover.py end refuses a row filed elsewhere. Beat: shown cost equals transcript sum (R7: 9.8 vs 24.5).
- **C2 edge checks on all (1%):** the five R7 checks run on every cast person and pose, kittested on Kemi and Blair too. Beat: zero Sam notes an existing check covers (R7: 7).
- **C3 pose command (1.5%):** joint targets plus reference photo give the standard sheet; refuses with no reference or a locked-pose diff not "none". Beat: Sam's "correct" within two sheets per new pose (R7 'apart': three).
- **C4 stall stop (0.3%):** in propose.py and the handover guard. Beat: spend before the kept method (R7: about 15.6%).
Total about 3.8% once, own job, against 15.6% lost in R7. Reviewed at Kemi's seated test (solid-body-default's next step).
**Sam would notice:** true costs at once, fewer sheets, no edge note given twice.
