# Proposal: Using the gifted 110k TikTok account to grow CranbriJoos

Status: open

## Problem
Sam was given a second TikTok account with 110k+ followers. Nine days later it still does nothing for CranbriJoos, his
main channel. TikTok's built-in way of linking two accounts' videos (Collaborate) isn't open to him. Plain reposts from
the gifted account have brought CranbriJoos nothing he can see. Sam asked whether Stitch or Duet could make it work, and
wants the whole question answered properly: how (or whether) this account can help the channel grow, without harming
either account.

## Facts
- Sam, 14 Nov (notes/ledger/sam/phxjozvs.md): the account was gifted by a man who liked Blessed. It holds funny animal
  compilation videos he made to amuse his daughter, and he no longer needs it. It is now tied to Sam's email, with no
  phone number. The last post was 18 Aug 2026. Some posts have thousands of views, others millions. 110k+ followers.
  It was in a 48-hour security cooldown for a new device.
- Sam, 23 Nov (notes/ledger/sam/jxpsvwma.md and this chat): "still trying to find a way to work for me". Collaborate
  "isn't available to me". Reposts "don't seem to do anything for me at all". He asked whether watching his own videos
  back and forth from the gifted account helps; this chat answered no (repeat self-views count little, and look like
  fake engagement).
- CranbriJoos: TikTok main platform, YouTube Shorts second (guides/job-packs.md, "My accounts"). The channel is
  topical absurdist news satire; every film is new (CLAUDE.md).
- TikTok terms of service, as quoted by third-party trackers (conductatlas.com; TikTok's own page not reached, so
  unchecked against the live text): "Do not give others access to your account, or transfer your account to anyone
  else, without our permission."
- TikTok community guidelines, Integrity and Authenticity (tiktok.com/community-guidelines): several accounts are
  allowed for authentic creative purposes; artificially boosting engagement and deceptive multi-account use are not.
- "Unoriginal content" (third-party blogs only, unchecked): videos reposted from other creators without real new value
  are kept off the For You feed; one creator-news report says Duets and Stitches are counted as unoriginal for Creator
  Rewards (TikTok's payment scheme). If the gifted account's compilations are other people's clips, that applies to it.
- Duet / Stitch (creator guides, unchecked): a Duet or Stitch is a new post on the account that makes it, shown to its
  followers and on the For You feed, with a tappable link to the original video and its account. No source documents a
  reliable follower gain for the original account.
- Changing an account's subject (creator guides, unchecked figures): TikTok re-tests new-subject posts on fresh viewers;
  early reach often drops (one vendor estimates 30-50%); followers who came for the old subject drift away.
- Unknown: the gifted account's real audience (country, age, how many followers are still active), how its videos
  perform now, whether its old clips were the man's own footage, and the exact results of Sam's reposts.
