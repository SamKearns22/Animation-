## The real decision
Not "which TikTok button links two accounts", but: is this audience worth anything to a UK news-satire channel, and if so, what is the one use that brings CranbriJoos real followers without risking either account? Bigger levers than the button: whether the audience matches at all, and the films' own reach to strangers, which already supplies over 90% of views.

## Root cause
Reposts and links pass viewers across only when those viewers want the content. The account's followers signed up for kids' animal clips, have seen nothing for three months, and TikTok shows posts to followers only as a first test before strangers. So the 110k is mostly a mismatched, cooling audience; reposts failed because a repost is a tiny feed item to people who did not ask for satire, not because the wrong button was used. Stitch or Duet would hit the same wall.

## Approaches weighed
1. Stitch/Duet each film from the gifted account. Cheap; adds a tappable credit, but same mismatched audience; Duets/Stitches may count as unoriginal (unchecked). Weak.
2. Rebrand the gifted account into a CranbriJoos sister channel (satire clips, trailers). Reframes, but changing subject loses reach (vendor estimate 30-50%, unchecked) and followers; the films get no new content, only a second, weaker shop window.
3. Learn first: read the account's own audience screens, then decide by a rule set in advance.
4. Reframe: leave TikTok cross-linking aside; the account's value may be as an animal-comedy outlet for new original animated pieces aimed at its existing audience, each ending with the CranbriJoos sign-off. New content, so it costs film budget; only worth it if the audience is active and adult enough.
5. Do nothing and keep the account safe. Honest, but drops a possible asset untested.

Chosen: 3, with 1 or 4 as the single change it may unlock.

## Proposal
**Learn (changes nothing).** Sam sends phone screenshots of the gifted account's TikTok Studio: Followers tab (countries, age, gender, active hours), Overview (last 60 days views, follower trend), and the stats of each repost he made (views, profile views). One Sonnet chat files them in story/metrics.md and writes the reading. Cost: about 1% of a week, outside any film.

**Decision rule, fixed now:**
- If under about a third of followers are UK/US adults (18+), or recent views are near zero: Claude says plainly the account cannot help the satire channel; it is kept safe (phone added, no posting) and nothing is built.
- Otherwise one change only, chosen by which fits the audience: (a) one Stitch per new film from the gifted account, or (b) approach 4. Written into guides/tiktok-design.md, Posting, as a standing step with the posting checklist enforcing it.

**Number to beat:** CranbriJoos new followers per 1,000 views on films with the step, against the last five films without it (baseline about 1.4 to 1.9), plus the profile views each gifted post sends (TikTok Studio). Reviewed when Sam declares each film complete; removed if it does not beat the baseline over the next three films.

**What Sam would notice:** one request for screenshots now; then either a plain "it can't help, here's why" or one extra posting step.
