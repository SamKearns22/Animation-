None that states a remedy. The frame leans on Sam's own question (Stitch or Duet) and on the "changing subject" figures,
which hint at turning the account over to CranbriJoos; both are weighed below, not assumed.

## The real decision
Not "can Stitch or Duet link two accounts", but "is a 110k audience of animal-video followers worth anything to a UK
satire channel whose views come from strangers on For You, and at what risk to CranbriJoos". Bigger levers than any
linking feature: each film's own For You performance (watch time, shares) and the cross-platform breakouts already seen
(Facebook, Instagram).

## Root cause
Followers bring reach only when they see and act on a post. TikTok shows a post mainly to strangers who match its
subject; an old follower base built for animal clips, idle since August, is a poor match for UK satire, so posts that
point at CranbriJoos (reposts) reach few people who would follow. Reposts failed because a repost is shown to the
reposter's followers' Following feed, which the record shows carries roughly nothing for us. Stitch and Duet fail the same
way: the new post is judged on the gifted account's For You, with only a small tappable link back.

## Approaches weighed
1. Stitch/Duet CranbriJoos films from the gifted account. Same audience mismatch as reposts; may count as unoriginal.
2. Post each CranbriJoos film natively there too. Two accounts competing with one video; duplicate-content and
   multi-account risk to the main channel.
3. Reframe: turn the gifted account into a sister channel with its own new satire, pointing to @cranbrijoos. Costs
   film budget and resets its reach anyway (new subject).
4. Learn first: read the account's own analytics and the repost results, check the risk, then decide with numbers set in
   advance.
5. Leave it as a protected spare, used for nothing that ties it to CranbriJoos.
Best for Sam's aims: 4, because 1-3 each spend Sam's time or risk the main channel on an audience nobody has measured.

## Proposal
**Learn (changes nothing):**
1. Sam sends four screens from the gifted account's TikTok Studio: followers' countries and ages, follower activity,
   last 60 days of views, and each repost's views and profile taps. A routine chat (Sonnet) logs them as a "Gifted
   account" snapshot in story/metrics.md.
2. The chat sets them against these fixed bars, written before the screens arrive: at least 40% UK, at least half aged
   18-34, and a recent post reaching at least 1% of its followers (1,100 views).
3. Safety, no posting: Sam adds his phone number and two-step login to the account, so a lost email cannot lose it.
**Change after, one at a time, only if the bars are met:** native CranbriJoos posts on the gifted account (approach 3,
renamed and rebranded), one new film each, each ending on "Follow @cranbrijoos". Number to beat: CranbriJoos new
followers within 48 h of each post above its last-five-film median. Reviewed at each film's end-of-film review. If the
bars fail: approach 5, and the answer to Sam is a plain "it can't help yet, here's why".
Cost: about 1% of a week for the logging chat (unchecked estimate); nothing from the 15% film budget. Sam would notice
one request for screenshots, then a yes/no with numbers.
