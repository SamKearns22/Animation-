# Proposal: what to measure next, before changing how films are made

Status: open (replay case 2)

## Problem
To choose which changes would cut the cost of making films, we need to know where the cost goes. So far two chats
(Protest Tiers chats 29 and 31) have been measured step by step. Decide what to measure next (which chats, how many,
which films, how) so that the changes chosen afterwards target the real costs.

## Facts
- The two bills are in the record (estimates from step counts).
- Films with ledgers: Blessed (about 80% of a week, older tools), Remakes (about 51%, older tools), Protest Tiers (29
  chats so far, made with the newest tools and rules); the ledgers are in notes/ledger/.
- Protest Tiers chats differ by kind of work: planning, building cast and places, building moves, fix rounds,
  animatic renders, handovers (their titles are in its ledger).
- Reading one chat's full record costs roughly 0.1-0.2% of a week (unchecked estimate: about 3-4% for all 29).
- Sam's own messages so far are in the record.
