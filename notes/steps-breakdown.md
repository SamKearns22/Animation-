Finding: chat size is fine (median ~100 steps, steps ~30% cheaper than Milburn). The money goes on one thing per
film the kit could not yet do, learned by trial and error inside the film, with the film's full checks on every try.
GTA's shot 4 alone cost about 4x a whole film's budget. It got cheap only once it moved to a plain grey test space
(chat 32 on, Sam's order): the whole grey clip went from ~80 min to ~3 min a render.

Proposed (waiting for Sam's Y): at the plan stage, list every move or view the kit has never drawn; each is learned
first as a practice clip (grey figure, no film checks), before the film is built.
