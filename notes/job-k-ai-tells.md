## The report: what reads as "AI" (strongest first)

**1. The talking photo (strong).** A long talking shot where the head stays dead upright, centred and front-on and
only the mouth and eyes move. Why it reads as AI: it is exactly what AI "talking avatar" apps make from one still
picture, and viewers now know that look. Where: Islamocommunism 28-66 s (El-Sayed has 12 s unbroken, 34.7-46.5 s);
Ad-Vanced Notice at the desk. Fix: head acting, driven by Sam's own recording: a new head tilt every 1-2.5 s, reached
in three drawings with a little overshoot and then held still; a small nod on each stressed word; the shoulders
follow a drawing later. Example: the clip (left today, right proposed). Cost: about 1-1.5% to build into the figure
rig (the clip fakes it by turning the finished drawing; the real one turns the head inside the rig, so lines stay
sharp). Risk to the style: low; the deadpan face stays, the moves are small.

**2. Airbrushed shading (strong).** The kit's shading is a blurred dark band (jackets, necks, arms, the beard's
edge) and a blurred shadow under the feet, on flat colour with sharp black lines. Why: that smooth, glossy
"flat drawing plus airbrush" finish is the signature of AI cartoon pictures, and our own look already says "no
gradients". Where: every film (Vance's suit, El-Sayed's jacket). Fix: the same shadow shapes with a hard edge. Sheet
row 1. Cost: about 0.2% (one switch in the shared kit). Risk: low; on dark suits the hard band may need to be lighter.

**3. Mixed techniques (strong where it happens).** Photo-like grain and randomly scattered straight straw sticks,
a see-through glowing ring, a camera gliding at 24 frames a second while people move at 12, all next to flat cartoon
people. Why: AI pictures mix ways of drawing in one frame; one artist draws everything one way. Where: Elder
Swindon's barn floor (2-14 s) and aerial. Fix: textures drawn as a few flat shapes with outlines; camera moves on
the same 12 a second as the people; glows only for a parody that quotes them (the Elder Scrolls title can stay).
Pre-flight would flag photo grain behind flat people. Cost: about 0.3%. Risk: low.

**4. Copies and pasted-on patterns (medium).** Groups drawn from one copy (the ICE officers' identical vests, builds
and masks; rows of identical houses) and patterns laid flat like wallpaper (the driver's check shirt is a perfect
square grid that ignores his body). Why: exact repeats and patterns that don't bend are classic generated-picture
tells. Where: Ad-Vanced Notice 28-40 s and 22 s; Elder Swindon aerial. Fix: the crowds-from-behind rule becomes an
automatic check for any group (each person a different build, height and kit detail); patterns bend at the body's
edges and break at seams and folds. Cost: about 0.7%. Risk: low.

**5. Dead-centre, mirror-perfect people (medium).** Faces exactly mirrored (both brows, eyes and ears identical),
people square to the camera in the middle of the frame, arms straight down. Why: AI pictures default to centred,
symmetrical subjects; drawn characters lean, shift their weight, have one brow higher. Where: Ad-Vanced Notice beach
(2-15 s) and street; Islamocommunism close-ups. Fix: a little asymmetry per invented character (one brow a touch
higher, parting off-centre, one mouth corner), weight on one leg when standing, framing off-centre where TikTok's
screen allows. Sheet row 2 shows the tilt. Cost: about 0.5%. Risk: medium for real people: a measured likeness
(Vance) keeps its measured face; asymmetry only where the real face has it.

**6. Black lines on everything (medium-low).** Background lines are as black and heavy as the people's. Why: the
same line everywhere looks like one filter over the whole picture; animators draw sets with softer coloured lines so
the people stand out. Where: every interior (Oval Office curtains and lanterns, bookshelves). Fix: set lines in a
darker shade of their own colour; people keep the black line. Sheet row 3. Cost: about 0.3%. Risk: medium: of all
the fixes it changes the look most, so try it on one film first.

**7. Dead silence between lines (sound, medium-low).** Between lines the sound drops to digital zero (14-15 gaps a
film in Ad-Vanced Notice and Islamocommunism). Why: a real recording always carries a little room and breath;
total silence between lines is how generated voices sound. Fix: keep Sam's own room and breaths in the gaps, faded
low, not cut to zero; nothing added (rule S3's hiss lesson stands). Cost: about 0.2% and one listening test. Risk:
faint hiss could come back; the test decides.

**8. Typed lettering on signs (weak).** Shop signs and badges in clean computer fonts. Real signs are often clean
too, so this matters little. Not worth the cost now.

**Not tells (keep):** circle hands, flat colour, 12 drawings a second, the random blinks (already irregular), hard
cuts, the Hook Text in TikTok's own style, and above all Sam's own voice and timing, the strongest "made by a
person" signal the films have.
