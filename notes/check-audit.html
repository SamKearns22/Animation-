Check audit (R8)

Every check in the project, what it actually caught in the last three films (Milburn, Grand Theft Autism, Open Wounds), and what I suggest for each one. 26 Nov.

What I found

  • We have about 500 checks: 184 kit tests, about 140 checks the tools run on a film, 20 at every handover, 12 that run on every message or step, 60 inside old films' own code, and 77 rulebook rows.
  • You gave 24 notes on Open Wounds. For 22 of them no check could have caught it. For the other 2 a check was looking and missed (the doorway pop-in, the thick wolf lines).
  • In the same weeks the checks printed 372 fault lines. 361 of them (97%) were the same faults coming back unchanged, run after run, and never acted on. That noise hides the real faults.
  • 65 of 430 handovers were stopped. 37 of those stops were the checking machinery tripping over itself (kit tests still running, crashed or not run). 24 were the same cast sheet faults again, mostly the seated "no bum" test you deleted today.
  • What worked: every fault fixed inside the drawing tools has stayed fixed (a nod can no longer snap, the wolves take the people's line weight, mouths are drawn with open lines). Faults that were only caught by a check came back (the leg gap, the smirk, the 3/4 shirt ridge).

So my overall suggestion is fewer checks that catch mistakes and more limits inside the tools, so the mistake can't be drawn. That's your "not make this mistake in the first place".

Move into the kit Prevent, don't catch

A1. "Drawn over" (what is in front of what)

Open Wounds: 30 faults back unchanged on 11 runs. It counts the ground, walls, pipes and wood panelling as things standing in front of people.

Have the drawing order come from the plan's distances automatically, and never count flat backdrops as things in front (the places kit already does this for its own pieces). The film check then retires, and a kit test keeps the order right.

Lost: the check on every pre-flight.Added: automatic order by distance in the drawing engine.

Smarter Better tool or way of working

B1. "Say it once" for every picture check (the biggest win)

97% of fault lines were repeats.

A fault that comes back unchanged is shown once, with a still and one line. After it's answered it stays quiet until the drawing in that spot changes, and then it comes back as "Reopened", with the old and new stills side by side. This replaces the open "answered faults come back" proposal.

Lost: the long repeated lists.Added: one still per fault, and a short "Reopened" list.

B2. Pop-ins that understand doors

Flagged guests coming through the door 4 times unchanged, yet missed your note 15 (people appearing out of a blank void in the doorway).

Someone arriving through a door has to come from the room behind it, with real depth. That gets checked, instead of doorways being ignored. This is part of the open "checks that understand what the scene is trying to do" proposal, which is still waiting for your Y or N.

Lost: the blanket pass for doorways.Added: a door rule that reads the plan.
