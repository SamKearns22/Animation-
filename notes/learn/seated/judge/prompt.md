# R7 blind judge instructions (written down in chat 6, 25 Nov; earlier chats' wording was not kept, rebuilt from
the answer format of judge-c4-*.md). Replace TAG and LETTER.

You are judging cartoon drawings cold. You know nothing about how they were made; do not guess which is new or old.
Read two pictures with the Read tool:
- notes/learn/seated/judge/outlines-TAG.jpg (12 numbered black silhouettes of seated people, heads removed)
- notes/learn/seated/judge/look-TAG.jpg (12 numbered colour drawings of seated people)

Step 1 (outlines sheet). For each number 1-12: is this a woman or a man? Then how sure, 1 (pure guess) to 5 (certain),
and the cue you used. One line each: "N: woman|man, SURE, cue". Mark "(guess)" if 1-2.

Step 2 (colour sheet). Look ONLY below the waist: bottom, thighs, knees, shins, feet, how they sit on the chair. For
each number 1-12 give a score 1-5 for "would a stranger believe these seated legs without effort?" (5 = completely
believable, 4 = believable with a small fault, 3 = readable but clearly wrong somewhere, 2 = wrong, 1 = broken), the
view (front / three-quarter / side / crossed legs), and every fault you see in plain words (lumps, tubes, floating feet,
legs passing through each other or the chair, a missing bottom, merged legs, knees that look like balls...).
One line each: "N: SCORE, view; faults".

Step 3. The three worst numbers, worst first, one line each with why.

Write your answers, exactly in that format with headings "## Step 1", "## Step 2", "## Step 3", to
notes/learn/seated/judge/judge-TAG-LETTER.md and reply with only "done".
