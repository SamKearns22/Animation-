# The body from behind, close (Patriots 5 chat 9, shot 6)
Left: chat 8's draft (handover note: "his neck from behind is long and thin, his arms read 'hands up' instead of
palms on the sill"). Right: chat 9.
What the project had to learn:
1. **The neck from behind** is two thick columns under the skull (about two thirds of the head's width at the nape)
   that widen at the foot into the slope of the shoulders (the trapezius). A straight stalk reads as a stick because
   no face frames it. Shared: views.BACK_NECK / views.back_neck(y), used by the body, the old heads and the house
   heads (turnhead.neck_over); the back collars (suit, tracksuit) wrap its flare. Kittest back_neck_flares.
2. **A hand resting on something drawn in true perspective** must land on it on screen. The rig draws a body flat at
   one scale, the set in perspective, so a 3D target "on the sill" floated: hands held up by the ears. Shared:
   views.hands_onto (contact by screen: moves each hand's target until the drawn hand sits on the set's point, within
   a pixel). Kittest hands_onto_lands.
3. Seen from close behind, the man stands to one side of the window so the person outside is not hidden.
